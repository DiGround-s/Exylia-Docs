---
title: "Tokens"
description: "Creation and edit tokens: the two balances per kind of custom cosmetic, how a rank or a store hands them out, and when they are checked and spent."
---

What limits a player is not how many custom cosmetics they hold, but how many they were given the
right to make. That right is a token, and there are two of them:

| Kind | Buys |
|---|---|
| `create` | writing one new custom tag or custom colour |
| `edit` | changing one they already made |

Balances live in `chatcosmetics_tokens`, one row per player, per cosmetic type, per kind. Nothing
about them appears in `config.yml`: they are player data, not configuration.

## Deleting refunds nothing

A player who deletes a creation gets nothing back. That is the whole point of counting creations
rather than possessions: a player handed three creations makes three, and the fourth costs another
token however many they delete in between. A refund would turn one purchase into an unlimited licence
to make and unmake for ever.

## Four kinds, each with its own two balances

Every kind a player writes by hand has its own pair:

| Type | Is |
|---|---|
| `customtag` | a tag they wrote |
| `customcolor` | a colour for what they write |
| `customnick` | a colour for their name |
| `customrank` | the colour their rank paints their name with |

Creations of custom colours do not pay for custom tags. A server selling both is selling two things.

## Giving them out

```
/cca tokens give  <player> <type> <create|edit> <amount>
/cca tokens take  <player> <type> <create|edit> <amount>
/cca tokens set   <player> <type> <create|edit> <amount>
/cca tokens check <player>
```

`<type>` must be one of the four kinds a player makes by hand. A catalogue type such as `tag` or
`font` is refused with a message saying so — nobody writes one of those, so there is nothing a token
could buy. Tab completion only offers the four.

`take` never takes a balance below zero, and `set` writes it outright. `check` prints every balance a
player holds, by type, and works on somebody offline.

<Callout type="note" title="The target may be offline">
A balance is a row of its own, so it is written and waits for them, exactly the way a grant does. A
store callback that fires while the buyer is logged out still works.
</Callout>

Three creations and five edits of a custom colour, for a player who just bought them:

```
/cca tokens give Notch customcolor create 3
/cca tokens give Notch customcolor edit 5
```

A rank hands them out the same way: run the command from whatever grants the rank — a LuckPerms
group's command list, a crate, the store's callback.

<Callout type="warning" title="There is no permission node that carries a balance">
Deliberately. A node cannot be spent: a player holding it would be able to make one custom colour or
a thousand, and taking the node away later would not undo what they already wrote. A balance is a
number that goes down.

There is a node for whether a player may make one **at all** —
`exyliachatcosmetics.customtag.create`, and the same shape for the three colour kinds — but it only
opens the door. Each creation still costs a token.
</Callout>

## Spending them

A player sees what they have left with `/cc tokens`, which lists both balances of every kind, and on
the MAKE ONE button of a custom browser, which reads `%create_tokens%` and `%edit_tokens%` for the
kind on screen.

| Where | Costs |
|---|---|
| MAKE ONE in the browser, and `/cc customtag create`, `/cc customcolor create`, `/cc customnick create`, `/cc customrank create` | one `create` of that kind |
| Shift right click on a row the player made | one `edit` |
| Drop key on a row the player made | nothing — and refunds nothing |

The balance is **checked before the questions are asked**, so nobody writes out a tag only to be told
they could never have afforded it. It is **spent after the last answer**, once the text and colour
have passed their rules and the name is not already taken, so a refused tag costs nothing. The spend
happens before the row is written, so a second click while the first is still saving finds the
balance already short rather than reading the same number twice.

A creation that is changed keeps its row number. What changed is what it reads, not which cosmetic it
is — so a tag that was worn stays worn, and a starred colour stays starred.

`/cca customtag set <player> <text> [color]` writes one for a player without spending anything. The
text rules still apply, since they are what keeps a slur out of chat.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Is |
|---|---|
| `%ecc_tokens_customcolor_create%` | what is left to make custom colours |
| `%ecc_tokens_customtag_edit%` | what is left to change custom tags |

The shape is `%ecc_tokens_<kind>_<create\|edit>%`, so the same two exist for all four kinds. Useful
on a scoreboard, or in the description of the shop entry that sells them.

In menus, the same two per kind are readable on any screen —
`%customcolor_create_tokens%`, `%customtag_edit_tokens%` — and inside a browser the bare
`%create_tokens%` and `%edit_tokens%` mean the kind currently on screen. See
[Menus](/docs/exyliachatcosmetics/menus).
