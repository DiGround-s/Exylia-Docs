ExyliaChatCosmetics is two things in one jar, and you can run either half on its own.

The **cosmetics** are what a player wears in chat: a tag in front of their name, a colour on the name,
a colour on their rank's prefix, a colour on what they type, a shadow under it, a font, a decoration
or two. They pick them from a menu, they own them through a permission or a grant that can expire, and
they save the combinations they liked as loadouts.

The **chat module** is off by default, and turning it on makes this plugin the server's chat:
channels, formats, private messages, a filter that reads English and Spanish, mentions, emojis,
`[item]` renders and an announcer. With it on the cosmetics land inside the line directly, with no
placeholder round-trip.

## The catalogue you get

Nothing here is a placeholder for content you have to write. The shipped files are the content.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `tags.yml` | **180 tags** in seven tabs — symbols, animated sprites, particles, item and block icons, interface icons, flags as head skins, premium |
| `nick-colors.yml` | **90 nick colours** in six tabs |
| `chat-colors.yml` | **96 chat colours** in six tabs |
| `shadow-colors.yml` | **90 shadow colours** in six tabs, answering the chat colour tabs |
| `rank-colors.yml` | **77 rank colours** in six tabs, including animated ones and a staff tab |
| `fonts.yml` | **18 fonts** in four tabs |
| `modifiers.yml` | bold, italic, underlined, strikethrough and obfuscated |
| `animations.yml` | **18 animations** across six kinds of movement |

They are ordinary YAML. Delete what you do not want, write your own, and `/cca reload` picks it up.

## A tag is not a second rank

Every tag the plugin ships is a **single glyph** — a symbol, a sprite, a particle, an item icon, a
flag drawn as a head. No brackets, no words, nothing that reads as a rank somebody bought.

<Callout type="note" title="Ranks stay where they are">
Rank colours repaint the prefix and suffix your permission plugin already writes; they never invent
one and never touch the name. A player wearing a rank colour is the same rank they were, in a colour
they chose. See [Colours](/docs/exyliachatcosmetics/colors).
</Callout>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Spigot runs the cosmetics through the legacy hook; the chat module needs Paper. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required | `ExyliaLib` |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI`, `LuckPerms` (rank colours need it), Redis for several servers |

<Callout type="note" title="1.21.9 for sprites and heads">
Tags built from sprites, particles and head skins are object components, which arrived in Minecraft
1.21.9. An older client shows those tags as the text they are written in rather than failing — but
the SYMBOLS tab, and everything about colours, fonts and the chat, works on 1.21 the same as on the
newest release.
</Callout>

## Conventions

- A cosmetic is named `type:id` everywhere — `tag:mvp`, `chat_color:aurora`, `customtag:42`.
- Keys are normalised to `a-z 0-9 _ -` in lower case, so `MVP` and `mvp` are the same id.
- Colours use ExyliaLib palette tokens (`{primary}`, `{highlight}`, `{muted}`), which follow your
  server's `colors.yml` rather than fixed hex values.
- Durations are ExyliaLib's: `30m`, `2h`, `14d`, `1w`, decimals allowed.
- Values marked **default** are what the plugin writes on the first start. An existing file is kept.
