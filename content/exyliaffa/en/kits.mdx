A kit belongs to an arena. It stores the full inventory, armour and offhand exactly as you had them
when you saved it from the panel.

## Fields

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | generated | The kit's internal identifier. It is what `/ffa resetkit <kitId>` takes. |
| `arenaId` | — | The arena it belongs to. Kits are not shared between arenas. |
| `name` | — | Name shown in the selector. Accepts colours. |
| `iconMaterial` | `CHEST` | Icon in the selection menu. |
| `permission` | empty | Permission required to use it. Empty means available to everyone. |
| `priority` | `0` | Order in the selector. Higher priority, higher up. |

## Hand-out modes

The mode is decided by the arena, not by the kit.

<Cards>
  <Card title="SELECTABLE">
    With more than one kit available, the selector opens on entry. The player picks, and that is the
    kit they respawn with for the rest of the session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RANDOM">
    The plugin hands out one at random among the kits the player has permission to use. Ideal for
    chaos arenas or to force variety.
  </Card>
</Cards>

If there is only one kit, no menu shows: it is handed over directly.

## Layout editor

Every player can rearrange a kit to taste without changing its contents. The layout is theirs and is
stored in the database.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    `/ffa kiteditor` opens the selector of editable kits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rearrange">
    Move items to the slots you want. While editing you are invulnerable and cannot break or place
    blocks, drop or pick up items, interact or fire projectiles: the kit is only reordered, never
    changed in contents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    `/ffa savekit` stores the arrangement. From then on, every time you receive that kit it arrives
    ordered that way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discard">
    `/ffa cancelkit` leaves edit mode without saving. `/ffa resetkit <kitId>` deletes your layout and
    returns to the original order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="An admin can reset it too">
`/ffaadmin resetlayout <player> <kitId>` deletes another player's saved layout. Useful when a kit
changes contents and the old layout no longer makes sense.
</Callout>

## Permission-gated kits

A kit's `permission` field works like an arena's: empty and anyone uses it; set and only holders of
that node do.

In `RANDOM` mode, kits the player is not allowed to use **do not enter their draw**. That gives a
premium rank a wider spread of kits without needing a separate arena.

## Refilling the kit after a kill

This is an arena setting, `resetKitAfterKill`. When on, killing refills the killer's kit to its
original state: same potions, same food, same durability.

<Callout type="tip">
In nodebuff arenas it pairs with `healOnKill` so that a clean kill leaves the winner at full again.
Without it, the first player to string two kills together dominates the arena by attrition.
</Callout>
