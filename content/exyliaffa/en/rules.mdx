---
title: "Arena rules"
description: "The twenty-one switches that define how an arena is played."
---

Every arena stores its own rules block. They are edited from the **Rules** menu inside the arena's
settings in `/ffaadmin`, and apply live: no restart, and players do not need to rejoin.

## Combat

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `pvp` | `true` | Allows damage between players. Off, the arena becomes a lobby. |
| `fallDamage` | `true` | Fall damage inside the arena. |
| `healthRegeneration` | `true` | Natural health regeneration. Turn it off in nodebuff-style arenas. |
| `foodDecrease` | `true` | Hunger drain. Off, food stops mattering. |
| `healOnKill` | `true` | Fully heals the killer after a kill. |
| `clearEffectsOnKill` | `false` | Clears the killer's potion effects after a kill. |

## Building

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `blockBreak` | `true` | Allows breaking blocks. |
| `blockPlace` | `true` | Allows placing blocks. |
| `playerBuildOnly` | `false` | Only player-placed blocks can be broken. Protects the original map. |
| `temporaryBlocks` | `false` | Placed blocks disappear on their own. |
| `temporaryBlocksSeconds` | `0` | How many seconds those blocks last before disappearing. |
| `reGiveBlocks` | `false` | Returns the block to the inventory when it disappears or breaks. |
| `interact` | `true` | Allows interacting with chests, buttons, doors and the like. |

<Callout type="tip" title="The clean-arena combination">
`playerBuildOnly: true` + `temporaryBlocks: true` + `temporaryBlocksSeconds: 8` +
`reGiveBlocks: true` is the classic recipe: players can use blocks to fight, the map does not
degrade, and nobody runs out of blocks.
</Callout>

## Items

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `itemDrop` | `true` | Allows dropping items. |
| `itemPickup` | `true` | Allows picking items up. |
| `dropItemsOnDeath` | `false` | A dying player drops their inventory. |
| `enderPearlCooldown` | `0` | Seconds between pearls. `0` disables the cooldown. |
| `goldenAppleCooldown` | `0` | Seconds between golden apples. `0` disables it. |

<Callout type="warning" title="dropItemsOnDeath and kits">
Turning on `dropItemsOnDeath` with kits that refill on death fills the floor with duplicates. Use it
only in arenas where loot is part of the game.
</Callout>

## Per-arena anti free-hit

| Arena rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `antiFreeHit` | `false` | Enables the 1v1 link in this arena. |
| `antiFreeHitHitsToLink` | `3` | Hits traded between two players before they count as linked. |
| `antiFreeHitIsolateLinkedPlayers` | `false` | Two linked players stop taking damage from third parties until the link ends. |

The link expires on its own once the seconds set in `anti-freehit.hit-reset-window-seconds` of
`config.yml` pass with neither player hitting the other. The full system is covered in
[Combat](/docs/exyliaffa/combat).
