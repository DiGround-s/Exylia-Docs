---
title: "Player settings"
description: "The ten switches each player controls from /ffa settings."
---

Every player decides what they see. Settings are stored in the database and survive reconnects and
restarts.

They are opened with `/ffa settings`.

## The settings

| Setting | Internal key | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| Scoreboard | `scoreboard` | Shows or hides the in-arena sidebar. |
| Spawn waypoints | `waypoints` | Client markers over each spawn of the arena. |
| Combat bar | `combat_bar` | The combat tag countdown above the hotbar. |
| Link bar | `linked_bar` | The bar with your 1v1 opponent: name, hearts, ping and time. |
| Combat messages | `combat_msgs` | The notices for entering and leaving combat. |
| Streak notifications | `ks_notifs` | Notices about your own streak. |
| Streak broadcasts | `ks_broadcasts` | Announcements of everyone else's streaks. |
| Kill broadcasts | `kd_broadcasts` | Other players' kill and death messages. |
| Opponent glow | `glow` | The outline on the player you are linked with. |
| Spectator hint | `spec_hint` | The action bar hint in spectator mode. |

All start switched on, except the scoreboard, which starts according to `scoreboards.yml`.

## A globally disabled setting cannot be switched on

If an admin disables the sidebar in `scoreboards.yml`, the player's scoreboard setting stops having
any effect: the global decision wins. The menu reflects that rather than letting someone switch on
something that will not appear.

<Callout type="note" title="Why this matters">
It is the difference between "the player has it off" and "the server does not offer it". Without that
distinction, a player could enable an option and conclude the plugin is broken because nothing
happens.
</Callout>

## Spawn waypoints

With the setting on, every spawn of the arena is drawn as a red marker on the player's client. The
markers belong to this plugin: they neither overwrite nor delete the ones your lobby plugin draws,
and they come down when leaving the arena or when ExyliaFFA disables.

A spawn with no assigned location draws nothing.

## Opponent glow

When the 1v1 link is active in the arena and the player has `glow` on, the opponent is outlined. It
is sent by packets through packetevents, which means:

- It uses no scoreboard teams, so it does not clash with your rank plugin's prefixes.
- Only the linked player sees it, not the whole server.
- It disappears the moment the link expires.

## Customising the menu

The menu is a YAML file like the rest: `menus/user/settings.yml`, with its Spanish translation in
`menus/user/es/settings.yml`. You can change icons, slots, texts and sounds without touching code.

<Callout type="tip">
If you add or move items, keep the action keys each button already carries: they are what tie the
button to the setting it toggles.
</Callout>
