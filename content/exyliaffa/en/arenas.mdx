title: "Arenas"
description: "Every field on an arena, what it does, and where it is stored."
---

An arena is a row in the database, not a block of YAML. It is created and edited from `/ffaadmin`,
and each change writes a new row: nothing is mutated in shared memory, so two admins touching the
same arena at once cannot leave it half-written.

## Fields

<Field name="id" type="text, max 64">
The arena identifier, chosen by whoever created it. It is what goes in `/ffa join <id>` and in
placeholders. It cannot be changed afterwards.
</Field>

<Field name="displayName" type="text, max 128">
The name menus show. Accepts colours and palette tokens.
</Field>

<Field name="iconMaterial" type="material" defaultValue="DIAMOND_SWORD">
The icon in the arena selector.
</Field>

<Field name="maxPlayers" type="integer" defaultValue="50">
How many players fit inside at once. Spectators do not take a combat slot, but they do count towards
the occupancy placeholder.
</Field>

<Field name="kitMode" type="SELECTABLE | RANDOM" defaultValue="SELECTABLE">
`SELECTABLE` lets players pick a kit on entry when there is more than one. `RANDOM` hands out one at
random among the kits that player is allowed to use.
</Field>

<Field name="resetKitAfterKill" type="boolean" defaultValue="false">
When on, every kill refills the killer's kit back to its original state.
</Field>

<Field name="region" type="two corners">
The arena bounds. Without a region, the arena cannot register as a playable session.
</Field>

<Field name="protectedRadius" type="integer, blocks" defaultValue="0">
Protection radius around each spawn. `0` disables protection.
</Field>

<Field name="protectedShape" type="SPHERE | CUBOID" defaultValue="SPHERE">
The shape of that radius.
</Field>

<Field name="protectedMode" type="NORMAL | FULL_HEIGHT" defaultValue="NORMAL">
`FULL_HEIGHT` extends protection from bedrock to sky, which stops anyone from standing above spawn to
hit whoever appears.
</Field>

<Field name="permission" type="text" defaultValue="empty">
Permission required to join. Empty means anyone can. This is the mechanism for premium arenas.
</Field>

<Field name="regenerationTime" type="seconds" defaultValue="0">
How often the arena's schematic is restored. `0` disables automatic regeneration.
</Field>

<Field name="priority" type="integer" defaultValue="0">
Ordering in menus and in arena selection. Higher priority shows first.
</Field>

<Field name="enabled" type="boolean" defaultValue="false">
Whether players may join at all. A newly created arena stays disabled until you switch it on.
</Field>

<Field name="saveInventoryOnLeave" type="boolean" defaultValue="false">
Stores the inventory the player brought and gives it back on leave. Recommended where FFA coexists
with survival, or with a lobby that hands out items.
</Field>

<Field name="rules" type="rules block">
The twenty-one in-arena rules. See [Rules](/docs/exyliaffa/rules).
</Field>

## Spawn protection

Three fields combine: the radius, the shape and the mode.

| Combination | Result |
|---|---|
| Radius `0` | No protection. Spawn is just another point of the arena. |
| `SPHERE` + `NORMAL` | A sphere around spawn. The natural choice in flat arenas. |
| `CUBOID` + `NORMAL` | A cube. Useful when spawn sits on a square platform. |
| Either + `FULL_HEIGHT` | Protection ignores height: floor to sky. |

<Callout type="tip" title="Picking a radius">
Radii of 3 to 5 blocks are usually enough. A large radius turns spawn into a shelter and players
learn to fight on its edge.
</Callout>

## Paid arenas

An arena with a `permission` is a restricted arena. The menu still shows it, but joining requires the
permission. Combined with [kit](/docs/exyliaffa/kits) and [spawn](/docs/exyliaffa/spawns)
permissions, it lets you design tiered access without duplicating arenas.

```text
exyliaffa.arena.vip      → the "vip" arena
exyliaffa.kit.diamond    → the "diamond" kit in any arena
exyliaffa.spawn.tower    → the "tower" spawn of one arena
```

The nodes are whatever you write in each field: the plugin imposes no prefix.

## Live state

An arena that is enabled and has a region registers as a **session** when the server starts. The
session is what holds the list of living players, the spectators, the regeneration timer and the
scoreboard. Disabling an arena closes its session and returns everyone to the lobby.
