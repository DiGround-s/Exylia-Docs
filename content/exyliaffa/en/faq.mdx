---
title: "Frequently asked questions"
description: "The problems that come up most, with short answers."
---

## The plugin does not start

Check the console. If `ExyliaLib`, `FastAsyncWorldEdit` or `packetevents` is missing, the server
disables ExyliaFFA and names it. All three are required.

## The arena does not show in `/ffa`

An arena shows once it is **enabled**. To be playable it also needs a region, at least one spawn and
at least one kit. The settings panel flags what is missing.

## Players appear and are thrown out of the arena

The spawn sits outside the region. Stand inside the marked area and create the spawn again.

## The arena does not regenerate

Check two things: that `regenerationTime` is greater than `0`, and that the arena has a saved
schematic. Without one, the cycle runs and does nothing.

## `%players%` stopped working in my scoreboards

That is the bug fixed in version 1.0.6: the occupancy placeholder registered a global name so generic
that it swallowed every `%players%` on the server. It is now
`%exyliaffa_arena_players_<arenaId>%`, and `scoreboards.yml` is rewritten on update. If you have
other plugins with `%players%` lines, they work again.

## A placeholder shows verbatim on screen

Three causes, by frequency:

1. PlaceholderAPI is not installed.
2. The arena identifier is misspelled.
3. The requested position exceeds `leaderboard.max-entries`.

## My messages show `%prefix%` literally

That happens when a message is sent from a file that does not go through the plugin's prefix. Every
text in `messages.yml` does. After editing the prefix, run `/ffaadmin reload`.

## Kills are not counting

Most likely the anti-farmkill: by default it refuses more than three kills on the same victim within
two minutes, and any kill between two players on the same IP. Tune or disable the system in
`config.yml`.

## The kit does not refill after a kill

`resetKitAfterKill` is an **arena** setting, not a kit one. Turn it on in the arena's settings.

## A player says the scoreboard does not show

They may have it off in `/ffa settings`. If nobody sees it, check `scoreboards.yml`: `ffa.enabled` set
to `false` disables it server-wide and no personal setting can switch it back on.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. The plugin declares Folia support, and per-arena tasks are anchored to the arena's location
rather than to a global thread.

## Can I share arenas between servers?

Yes: point every `database.yml` at the same MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL. Arenas, kits, spawns,
statistics and settings live in the database, so they are shared. Note that schematic files live on
each server's disk.

## I changed `database.yml` and nothing happened

The database client is opened at boot. Restart the server; `/ffaadmin reload` does not replace it.
