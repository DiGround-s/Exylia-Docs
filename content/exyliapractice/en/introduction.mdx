ExyliaPracticeCore is a complete practice server. A player joins the lobby, picks a kit from the queue
menu, gets matched against somebody near their rating, fights in an arena that was pasted for that
match alone, and lands back in the lobby with their LP already updated.

It is the largest plugin in the ecosystem: **235 classes, 17 database tables, 61 menu screens and 15
commands**.

## What it does

<Cards>
  <Card title="Ranked matchmaking" href="/docs/exyliapractice/queue">
    A player queues for several kits at once. The search radius widens the longer they wait.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duels" href="/docs/exyliapractice/duels">
    Challenge a player directly, pick the kit, the arena and how many rounds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parties" href="/docs/exyliapractice/parties">
    FFA, split teams, or one party queued against another.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cloning arenas" href="/docs/exyliapractice/clones">
    One built arena becomes up to ten simultaneous copies in a void world.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nine ranks" href="/docs/exyliapractice/elo">
    Ember through Legend, with divisions, LP, placements and a demotion shield.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kit editor" href="/docs/exyliapractice/kit-editor">
    Players rearrange the kit's own items into named, saved loadouts.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## How a ranked match actually runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Queue">
    The player opens `/queue`, picks a category, then one or more kits. They stay in the lobby with an
    action bar showing the kits, the elapsed time and the rating band being searched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match found">
    Every second the plugin looks for two adjacent players in the queue whose ratings are within the
    wider of their two search radii, and who are not blocked by the [anti-boost
    checks](/docs/exyliapractice/elo#anti-boosting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arena">
    A free instance of one compatible arena is taken. If every one is busy, a **clone** is pasted from
    the arena's schematic into the clone world and used instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Countdown">
    Both players are teleported to the two spawns, given the kit, put in adventure mode and counted
    down — five seconds by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fight">
    The kit's rules become policies on the arena region: build, break, interact, PvP, item drop and
    pickup, fall damage, disappearing blocks. An action bar shows health, hits, combo, damage,
    accuracy and ping for both sides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result">
    ELO is applied to both the kit ladder and the global ladder, a row is written to the match
    history with both inventories, and the arena is regenerated from its schematic before the next
    match uses it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required | `ExyliaLib`, `FastAsyncWorldEdit` |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI`, `ViaVersion`, `Worlds`, `Apollo`, `FeatherServerAPI`, `floodgate` |

<Callout type="warning" title="FastAsyncWorldEdit is not optional">
Arena cloning and arena regeneration are both schematic operations. Without FAWE the plugin still
starts and says so once in the console, but an arena can never be enabled: two of its requirements
are the schematics it cannot save.
</Callout>

## What it is not

It does not handle FFA, events, the sandbox or cosmetics — those are separate plugins. What it does
do is hand a player over cleanly when one of them takes them: the lobby reacts to the shared claim
registry in ExyliaLib rather than being told, so a mode written next year needs no changes here.
