---
title: "Duels"
description: "Challenging a player directly, best-of-N rounds, and what a duel does that a queue match does not."
---

A duel skips matchmaking entirely: one player picks the opponent, the kit, the arena and how many
rounds, and the other accepts.

## Sending one

`/duel <player>` — or `/d`, or the **Duel** button on a player's row in a menu — opens a short chain
of screens:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Category">
    The kit categories, as everywhere else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kit">
    Only kits with **duel** enabled are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rounds">
    1, 3, 5, or **Custom** — which asks in chat for anything from 1 to 20.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arena">
    Optionally, a preferred arena from the kit's compatible list. It is tried first; if it is busy the
    rest of the list is used instead, so a preferred arena never causes a failure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both players get a message naming the kit and the round count.

## Answering one

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/duel accept [player]` | Accept. With no name, the first pending request. |
| `/duel deny [player]`, `/duel decline [player]` | Decline. |
| `/duel cancel` | Withdraw the request you sent. |

Requests expire after `duel.request-expiry-seconds` — 30 by default — and the sender is told when
theirs runs out.

<Callout type="note" title="One outgoing request at a time">
A player may hold only one pending request as sender, but may receive several. `/duel accept` with no
argument takes whichever is found first, so name the player when more than one is waiting.
</Callout>

## Requirements

A duel refuses when:

- Either player is not free — queued, in a match, editing a kit, spectating, or held by another
  plugin.
- The target has **duel requests** turned off in `/settings`.
- The sender already has a request pending.

Both players are claimed at the same moment when the duel is accepted. If the second claim fails, the
first is released and nothing starts — nobody ends up in a duel their opponent has walked out of.

## Rounds

A duel with more than one round is a **best-of**. The first player to win `rounds` rounds wins the
match.

Between rounds:

- Both get a *round won* / *round lost* title carrying the running score.
- After `duel.round-end-delay-ticks` — 80 by default, four seconds — the arena is **regenerated in
  place**, without being released.
- Everybody is revived, points are reset to zero, both are teleported back to their spawns, re-kitted,
  and the normal countdown runs again.

A round that ends in a draw restarts without giving anybody a win.

<Callout type="note" title="Rounds and points stack">
A kit with `points-to-win` in a duel means each round is itself a race to that point total. The
sidebar switches to the duel-with-points layout automatically, showing rounds and points side by side.
</Callout>

## ELO

A duel is ranked if the **kit** is ranked. There is no separate switch — a ranked kit gains and loses
LP through `/duel` exactly as it does through `/queue`.

<Callout type="warning" title="The anti-boost checks do not gate duels">
Same-IP protection and the recent-opponent cooldown are asked before a **queue** match is paired, not
before a duel is accepted. Two accounts can duel each other repeatedly for LP. The duel does record
the pair as recently matched, which blocks them from meeting through the queue for the next
`elo-security.opponent-cooldown-seconds`, but nothing stops the duels themselves. If that matters on
your server, turn **ranked** off on the kits you offer for duelling, or **duel** off on the ranked
ones.
</Callout>
