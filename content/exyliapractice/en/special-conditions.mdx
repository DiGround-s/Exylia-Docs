title: "Special conditions"
description: "Eight triggers, three actions, and the gamemodes you can build by combining them."
---

A special condition is a rule of the form *when this happens to a player, do that*. They live on the
kit, under `/epc` → **Manage Kits** → a kit → **Kit rules** → **Special conditions**.

## The eight triggers

| Trigger | Fires when the player |
|---|---|
| `TOUCH_WATER` | stands in water |
| `TOUCH_LAVA` | stands in lava |
| `TOUCH_END_GATEWAY` | stands in an end gateway |
| `TOUCH_END_PORTAL` | stands in an end portal |
| `TOUCH_NETHER_PORTAL` | stands in a nether portal |
| `STEP_ON_GOLD_PRESSURE_PLATE` | steps on a light weighted pressure plate |
| `FALL_INTO_VOID` | falls into the void |
| `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` | enters the [extra region farthest from their spawn](/docs/exyliapractice/extra-regions) |

All the block triggers are checked on the block the player's feet are in, and only when they change
block — standing still in lava fires once, not every tick.

## The three actions

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| `ADD_POINT` | The player gains a point. If that reaches `points-to-win`, they win the round or the match. |
| `LOSS_POINT` | The player loses a point. |
| `DEATH` | The player is eliminated — or, with `iron-plate-checkpoints` on and a checkpoint set, healed and sent back to it with 2 seconds of invulnerability instead. |

A trigger can carry **several** actions; each is applied in the order they were toggled on.

## What you can build with them

<Cards>
  <Card title="Sumo">
    `TOUCH_WATER` → `DEATH`. Kit with no items, PvP on, arena is a platform over water.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Capture">
    `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT`, two extra regions, `points-to-win: 3`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parkour race">
    `FALL_INTO_VOID` → `DEATH` with `iron-plate-checkpoints` on, so a fall returns you to the last
    heavy plate. `STEP_ON_GOLD_PRESSURE_PLATE` → `ADD_POINT` at the finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bridge">
    `FALL_INTO_VOID` → `DEATH`, `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT`, `build` on,
    `regenerate-after-point` on.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="note" title="Points can go below zero">
`LOSS_POINT` subtracts without a floor, so a player who keeps triggering it shows a negative score on
the sidebar. It costs them nothing beyond the display — a winner is only resolved from a positive
score — but pair `LOSS_POINT` with something that ends the round if you want it to be decisive.
</Callout>

## Interaction with revives

An eliminated player in a kit with `revive-time` above zero spectates for that many seconds and comes
back at their spawn, rather than ending the match. That is what makes a `DEATH` condition a penalty
instead of a loss, and it is how a bridge or a parkour kit runs to a point total rather than to a
single mistake.
