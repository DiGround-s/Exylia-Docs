Everything outside a match is the lobby's business: what a player is holding, who they can see, and
the four kinds of thing they can walk into.

## The three hotbars

`items/lobby.yml`, `items/party.yml` and `items/queue.yml` describe the hotbar a player carries in
each state. The right one is applied whenever their state changes.

Each entry is a slot, an item, and a list of commands:

```yaml
queue:
  slot: 0
  material: MACE
  name: "<gradient:#FF8C00:#FF4500>⚔ Queues</gradient>"
  lore:
    - "<#888888>Browse available queues"
  commands:
    - "any: player: queue"
```

The prefix before the first colon names the click kinds the line answers to — `left`, `right`,
`shift_left`, and so on, comma-separated — and `any` or no prefix means every click. The rest is a
command line as the library reads one, so `console:` works as well as `player:`.

The shipped lobby hotbar links out to `/ffa`, `/events` and `/rtp`, which belong to other plugins.
Remove the entries you do not run.

<Callout type="note" title="A player inside a PvP zone keeps their kit">
Hotbars are not reapplied to somebody standing in a PvP region — they are holding that zone's kit, and
overwriting it mid-fight would be worse than the wrong compass.
</Callout>

## Double jump

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `lobby.double-jump-enabled` | `true` |
| `lobby.double-jump-vertical-power` | `0.9` |
| `lobby.double-jump-forward-power` | `0.6` |
| `lobby.double-jump-cooldown-ms` | `500` |
| `lobby.double-jump-sound` | `ENTITY_ENDER_DRAGON_FLAP\|0.6\|1.8` |

It is off for anybody with `exyliapractice.lobby.fly`, since they are flying instead, and each player
can turn it off for themselves in `/settings`.

`lobby.effects` is a list of potion effects applied in the lobby, written as `NAME|LEVEL` — `SPEED|2`
by default.

## Visibility

Every player decides who they see in the lobby, from `/settings`:

| Setting | Default | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Show VIP** | on | Players with `exyliapractice.lobby.vip` |
| **Show party** | on | Your own party members |
| **Show others** | off | Everybody else |

Anybody with `exyliapractice.lobby.bypass` is always visible regardless. A player who is *not* in the
lobby — in a match, in a queue-loading state, held by another mode — is handled separately: the lobby
hides them from you and hides you from them.

## Jump pads

A jump pad is a set of block locations that launch whoever steps on them.

`/epc` → **Manage Jumpads**. Each pad has a display name, an enabled flag, a yaw (the direction of the
launch), a forward power, a vertical power, and any number of locations sharing all of it.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `jumpad.launch-cooldown-ms` | `500` | Per-player, so one pad cannot be spammed |
| `jumpad.only-lobby-area` | `true` | Pads only fire for players in a lobby state |
| `jumpad.launch-sound` | `ENTITY_SLIME_JUMP\|1.0\|1.2` | |

## Portals

A portal is a region that runs a command, teleports, or both, the moment a player walks into it.

`/epc` → **Manage Portals**. Each has a region, an optional command (typed without the leading slash,
run as the player) and an optional teleport destination. If both are set, the command runs first.

Portals only fire for players in a lobby state, so somebody being carried through by a match is not
teleported out of it.

## PvP zones

A PvP zone is a warm-up arena inside the lobby: walk in, get handed a kit, fight, walk out.

`/epc` → **Manage PvP Regions**. Each zone carries:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Region** | Where it is |
| **Kit items** | The loadout handed out on entry. Set from your own inventory. |
| **Enabled** | A disabled zone tells whoever enters and does nothing else |
| **Combat tag seconds** | How long after being hit a player counts as in combat |
| **Build / Break** | Whether blocks may be placed or broken inside |
| **Drop items** | Whether items may be dropped |
| **Natural regen** | Whether health regenerates |
| **Hunger depletion** | Whether food drains |

Entering saves the player's previous state, claims them as `IN_PVP_ZONE`, and hands them the zone's
kit. Leaving restores what they were doing and clears the combat tag.

Being hit inside a zone shows a combat-tag boss bar, configured under `pvp-regions.combat-tag-bossbar`
in `config.yml` with `%time%` for the seconds left.

<Callout type="note" title="A player in a PvP zone can still queue">
`IN_PVP_ZONE` is treated as a lobby-adjacent state throughout: they can join a queue, they see the
queue sidebar, and they see the teaser bar. Finding a match pulls them out of the zone.
</Callout>
