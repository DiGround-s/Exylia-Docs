An arena is a row in `practice_arenas`. Everything about it is edited from `/epc` → **Manage Arenas**.

## The two regions

This is the part that confuses people, so it is worth being exact.

| Region | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Playable region** | The area a match happens in. Leaving it kills you. It is what a **regeneration** restores between matches, and it is what carries the kit's rules as region policies. |
| **Copy region** | A larger box around the arena. It is what a **clone** is pasted from, and it is the origin every clone's coordinates are measured against. |

The copy region should contain the playable region. Walls, floor, decoration, the spectator platform
— anything that has to exist for a copy of this arena to look and play right.

<Callout type="note" title="Why the playable one is smaller">
Regenerating the whole copy region between every match would rebuild walls nobody touched. The
playable region is the part players can damage, so it is the only part restored.
</Callout>

## The four spawns

| Spawn | Required | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **Spawn 1** | Yes | The first side of every match |
| **Spawn 2** | Yes | The second side |
| **Spectator spawn** | No | Where a spectator lands |
| **Party FFA spawn** | No | The centre of a party FFA; players are scattered around it |

In a 1v1 or a party duel, players alternate between spawn 1 and spawn 2. In a party FFA everybody
starts around the FFA spawn within `party.ffa-spawn-radius` blocks — 7 by default.

## Protection radii

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Spawn protection radius** | Blocks may not be placed or broken within this many blocks of either spawn |
| **Extra region protection radius** | Same, around the centre of every extra region |

Both default to `0`, which means no protection. They only matter for kits that allow building.

## Schematics

Every arena owns two schematic files, named after its id:

| File | Saved from | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `practice_arena_<id>` | The copy region | Pasting clones |
| `practice_arena_<id>_playable` | The playable region | Regeneration between matches |

Both are saved automatically the moment you set the matching region, and the **Schematic** button
re-saves both. Re-save after any rebuild: a regeneration restores the file, not the blocks currently
standing.

## What blocks an arena from being enabled

The **Status** button refuses and lists what is missing:

- Spawn 1
- Spawn 2
- Playable Region
- Copy Region
- Copy Schematic (save from Copy Region)
- Playable Schematic (save from Playable Region)

<Callout type="warning" title="The two schematic entries are also how a missing FAWE shows up">
The check asks the schematic store whether the file exists. On a server without FastAsyncWorldEdit
the answer is always no, so the arena can never be enabled and the reason reads as a missing
schematic rather than a missing plugin. The console said the real reason once, at startup.
</Callout>

## Instances

One arena is one or more **instances**. The original — the built one, in your world — is instance
`<id>`. Every clone is `<id>_clone_<n>`. Each instance is independently `AVAILABLE`, `IN_USE`,
`REGENERATING` or `DISABLED`, and taking one is a compare-and-set, so two matches can never land in
the same arena.

When a match asks for an arena it goes through its kit's compatible list in order, taking the first
free instance of any of them. Only when every instance of every compatible arena is busy does it
shuffle the list and [clone one](/docs/exyliapractice/clones).

## Deleting an arena

Deleting removes the row, unregisters every clone's region and resets the clone counter. It does not
delete the schematic files and it does not touch the built arena in your world.

<Callout type="note" title="Kits keep the id">
A kit whose compatible list still names the deleted arena keeps naming it. The entry matches nothing
and is skipped; tidy it up from the kit's **Compatible arenas** screen.
</Callout>
