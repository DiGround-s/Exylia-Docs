---
title: "Match history"
description: "Every match, both inventories, and the sixty-day window it lives in."
---

Every finished match writes one row **per player** to `practice_match_history`. The two rows share a
`matchId`, which is what lets a player look at their opponent's side of the same fight.

## What a row holds

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| Identity | `matchId`, `season`, `playerUuid`, `opponentName`, `kitId`, `arenaId`, `matchMode` |
| Outcome | `result` (WIN / LOSS / DRAW), `ranked`, `playedAt`, `durationMs` |
| Rating | `eloBefore`, `eloChange`, `eloAfter`, and the same three for the global ladder |
| Your side | `kills`, `deaths`, `damageDealt`, `hitsDealt`, `hitsReceived`, `totalSwings`, `bestCombo` |
| Their side | The same seven, prefixed `opponent` |
| Inventory | `inventoryData` — the player's inventory as it was when the match ended |

A row is written for every non-cancelled match, ranked or not.

## Viewing it

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/history`, `/mh`, `/match-history` | Your own history |
| `/history <player>` | Somebody else's |
| `/history inv <matchId>` | The inventory you finished that match with |
| `/history invop <matchId>` | Your opponent's |

The menu opens on the category list, then the kits, then the matches — each row showing the result,
the LP change, the opponent, the kit and how long it took. Clicking one opens the two inventory views.

Five filters: **all**, **wins**, **losses**, **ranked**, **unranked**.

<Callout type="note" title="The last 300 matches">
A history query reads a player's 300 most recent matches, ordered by the database, and filters that
window in memory. A kit you have not played in a long time can therefore look emptier than it is — the
match is still in the table, it is just outside the window the menu reads.
</Callout>

Results are cached for two minutes per player, kit and filter.

## Retention

Match rows are deleted after **60 days**. The sweep starts five minutes after boot and runs hourly,
deleting the oldest first, in batches, entirely inside the database with a pause between batches so a
live match's inserts are not held up.

<Callout type="note" title="Deleting a match costs nothing statistically">
Every counter derived from a match — wins, damage, accuracy, combos, time played — was already
committed to the season and lifetime rows when the match ended. The history row is the replay, not the
source of truth.
</Callout>

The retention window is not a config key.

## Purging

`/epc` → **Data Management** → **Purge match history** deletes match rows outright, in larger batches
than the background sweep, since nothing is competing with it. It is irreversible and asks for
confirmation.

## The inventory snapshot

The inventory is captured when the match ends: 36 storage slots, 4 armour slots and the offhand,
serialised into the row. That is what `/history inv` renders — the actual items, durability and
enchantments included, as an unclickable menu.

Because it is captured at the end, a player who died holding nothing shows nothing. The snapshot is
what they had, not what the kit gave them.
