---
title: "Seasons"
description: "Rotating the ladder in two database writes, what survives it, and the restart that follows."
---

A season is a numbered period every stat row is stamped with. Rotating one closes the current season
and opens the next; nothing is copied, uploaded or deleted, so it finishes in milliseconds no matter
how many players the server has.

## The three tiers of stats

| Tier | Table | Reset by a rotation |
|---|---|---|
| **Season** | `practice_player_season_stats` | Effectively yes — the new season starts with no rows |
| **Lifetime** | `practice_player_lifetime_stats` | No, ever |
| **Match history** | `practice_match_history` | No; rows carry the season they were played in |

Nothing is destroyed by a rotation. The previous season's rows stay in the table under their season
number, which is what lets a season selector show old standings.

## Rotating

`/epc` → **Data Management** → **New Season**, then confirm. There is no command form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lock the queue">
    Matchmaking is held for the length of the two writes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close and open">
    The active season row is marked closed with an end timestamp; the next number is written as the
    new active season; the settings row's mirror of the number is updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch everybody over">
    Online players start writing into the new season immediately, without reconnecting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel boosts">
    Every personal and global [ELO boost](/docs/exyliapractice/elo#boosts) is cancelled — a boost
    belongs to the season that granted it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the web">
    Best-effort. Seasons are identified by number, so a late or retried notification converges to the
    same result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The restart

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `season.restart-after-rotation` | `true` | Restart the server once the rotation finishes |
| `season.restart-delay-seconds` | `15` | The countdown players are warned with |

With it on, the plugin drains the [web outbox](/docs/exyliapractice/web-api) up to three times, warns
everybody, waits out the countdown, and calls `Bukkit.spigot().restart()` — falling back to a plain
shutdown if that is unavailable.

The queue **stays locked** through the countdown on purpose: reopening it would let somebody start a
match the restart is about to interrupt.

<Callout type="note" title="Whether the server comes back is your supervisor's job">
`restart()` only relaunches the process when `restart-script` in `spigot.yml` points at a working
script. Otherwise it stops cleanly, and something outside Minecraft has to bring it back — which is the
right arrangement for a containerised server anyway.
</Callout>

With `restart-after-rotation` off, the queue reopens as soon as the two writes land and play continues
on the new season.

## The progress bar

`season.progress-bossbar` in `config.yml` is the bar shown while a rotation runs, with `%phase%` and
`%percent%`.

## Repair on boot

The plugin resolves the active season on every start and quietly repairs the two states that should
not happen:

- **No season at all** — a fresh install gets season 1.
- **Several marked active** — a rotation interrupted mid-write. The highest number wins and the rest
  are closed, with a warning naming each.
- **Every season closed** — a rotation that died between closing and opening. The newest is reopened
  rather than a new number invented.

## What a rotation does not do

It does not clear match history, it does not touch kit loadouts, it does not reset arenas, and it does
not upload anything synchronously. If you want the previous season's stats *gone* rather than archived,
that is **Data Management** → **Clear all stats**, which is a different and irreversible action.
