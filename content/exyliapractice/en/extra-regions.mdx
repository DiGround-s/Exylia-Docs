---
title: "Extra regions"
description: "Optional boxes inside an arena that become a target, a home, or both — the basis of capture-style kits."
---

An arena can carry any number of **extra regions**: boxes selected the same way the playable region
is, stored on the arena, cloned along with it. On their own they do nothing. They become meaningful
once a kit assigns them a role.

## Target and home

At the start of every match, each player gets their own view of the arena's extra regions, worked out
from where they spawn:

| Role | How it is chosen | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Target** | The extra region **farthest** from that player's spawn | At least one extra region |
| **Home** | The extra region **nearest** that player's spawn | At least two |

With exactly one extra region, everybody shares it as their target and nobody has a home. With two,
the players spawning at opposite ends get mirrored roles automatically — one player's target is the
other player's home.

## What each role does

**Home** is a wall. Walking into your own home region teleports you straight back to your spawn. It
exists so a player cannot camp their own objective.

**Target** fires the `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` [special condition](/docs/exyliapractice/special-conditions).
Whatever that condition is set to on the kit — add a point, lose a point, die — happens when the
player steps into the far region.

<Callout type="note" title="This is how a capture kit is built">
Two extra regions, one at each end of the arena. Set `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT` and
`points-to-win` to 3. Reaching the enemy's end scores; your own end bounces you back to spawn. No
extra plugin, no flags, no scripting.
</Callout>

## Setting them up

`/epc` → **Manage Arenas** → an arena → **Extra Regions**. Adding one opens the selection wizard; the
list screen removes them. They are stored with generated ids of the form
`practice_arena_<id>_extra_<n>` and no name of their own.

## Protecting them

The arena's **extra region protection radius** stops blocks being placed or broken within that many
blocks of every extra region's centre — the counterpart of spawn protection, for kits that allow
building.

## In clones

Extra regions are offset and re-registered with every clone, under
`practice_arena_<id>_clone_<n>_extra_<i>`. Roles are recomputed per match from each player's actual
spawn in that instance, so a clone behaves identically to the original.

<Callout type="warning" title="Order matters when you remove one">
Ids carry the index they were added at. Removing a region from the middle of the list does not
renumber the ones after it, and a region added later reuses an index that the removed one had. That
is harmless for gameplay — roles are computed from distance, never from the id — but it means an id
is not a stable name to build anything else on.
</Callout>
