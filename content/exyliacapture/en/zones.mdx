---
title: "Zones"
description: "Selecting where an event happens, and what each type needs."
---

Zones are cuboids, selected in game with the shared Exylia selection wand. There is no WorldEdit
dependency, and there are no spherical or polygonal zones.

## Selecting

The setup screen starts a guided flow: it hands you the wand, you mark the two corners, and the
selection is saved back onto the event. Backing out leaves the previous zone untouched.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event's setup screen">
    From `/captureadmin`, pick the event and open its configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the zone step">
    The flow explains what to select and gives you the wand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark two corners">
    Left click for one, right click for the other. The volume between them is the zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The selection is written to the event. Re-selecting later replaces it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each type selects

| Type | What is selected |
|---|---|
| `koth`, `koth_points_exclusive`, `koth_points_multi`, `koth_capture_points` | One cuboid |
| `conquest` | One cuboid per zone, added from the zone list screen |
| `payload` | A start location and an end location |
| `dtc` | One block |

Conquest zones are managed from their own list: add, edit name, colour, points and capture time, or
re-select the area of one.

## Being inside

Presence is re-evaluated as players move, and also on join, respawn, death and gamemode change. What
counts as playing:

| Situation | Counts |
|---|---|
| Alive, survival or adventure, inside the zone | Yes |
| Creative | No |
| Spectator | No |
| Dead | No |

A player who switches to creative inside a zone stops counting immediately, and one who switches back
starts counting again without leaving and re-entering.

## The outline

While an event runs, its zones are outlined with particles. It is configured globally, not per event:

```yaml title="config.yml"
particles:
  enabled: true
  interval-ticks: 40
  particle: WAX_ON
  view-distance: 48.0
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Draw the outline at all. |
| `interval-ticks` | `40` | Ticks between frames. 20 ticks is one second. |
| `particle` | `WAX_ON` | Any Bukkit particle name. |
| `view-distance` | `48.0` | Beyond this many blocks nothing is sent, because nothing would be drawn. |

<Callout type="tip">
`interval-ticks` is the cost knob. A large arena with several running events redraws every outline on
each frame; doubling the interval halves that work and is barely noticeable.
</Callout>

## Payload rails

Payload does not use a cuboid. It takes two locations and scans the rail path between them, walking
from rail block to neighbouring rail block. Both endpoints must be rails, and the path must be
connected — a gap means no path, and the cart has nowhere to go.

The `radius` setting decides how close a player must be to the cart to push it, measured from the cart
itself as it moves, not from a fixed zone.
