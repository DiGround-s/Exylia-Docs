---
title: "Clans"
description: "Which clan plugins are detected, what clan mode changes, and what happens without one."
---

Clan support is provided by ExyliaLib, not by ExyliaCapture, and it is detected automatically at
startup — there is no setting naming which plugin to use.

## What is detected

ExyliaLib probes for these eight, in this order, and uses the first one that is present and
reachable:

1. **FactionsUUID / Factions**
2. **HuskTowns**
3. **ZelTeams**
4. **RunithClans**
5. **UltimateClans**
6. **Kingdoms / KingdomsX**
7. **SimpleClans**
8. **ExyliaClans**

Any plugin can also register a bridge with ExyliaLib, and a registered bridge beats automatic
detection. That is the supported route for a clan plugin that is not on the list.

The console line at startup says which one was picked: `Clans: detected '<name>'`, or
`Clans: using registered bridge '<name>'`.

<Callout type="note" title="Order is priority, not preference">
The first plugin found wins, so a server running two clan plugins gets the one higher on the list.
Every provider reaches its plugin by reflection, so a clan plugin that is installed but whose API
cannot be reached is skipped with a console warning rather than taking the slot silently.
</Callout>

## What clan mode changes

`clan-mode` is a per-event setting on KOTH Points, KOTH Capture Points, Payload and DTC. Conquest is
clan-based always and has no toggle.

With it on:

| Behaviour | Individual | Clan mode |
|---|---|---|
| Who holds a zone | One player | The clan, so it survives one member leaving while another stays |
| Who scores | The player | The clan's total |
| Who wins | The player who reached the target | The clan that reached it |
| Who is paid | The player | One representative of the clan |
| Whose stats go up | The player | Every participating member of the clan |

A player without a clan is skipped: they cannot hold the zone and their points are not attributed.

<Callout type="note" title="No clan plugin, no clan mode">
Clan scoring requires both the setting and a detected provider. With the setting on and no provider,
the event falls back to individual scoring rather than refusing to run — except Conquest, whose zones
cannot register a capturer at all.
</Callout>

## Clan statistics

Clan statistics are tracked alongside player statistics, globally and per event: wins, points,
captures and leaderboard points. They feed `/capture clantop` and the
`%exyliacapture_top_clan_<n>_<stat>%` placeholders.

A win is recorded for the clan whenever a winning player belongs to one — that happens in both modes,
because the win recording looks up the winner's clan regardless of how the event was scored.
