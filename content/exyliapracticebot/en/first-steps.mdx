Two things are typed: one world name in a config file, and `/bot`. Everything else happens in a menu.

## Allow a world

A bot may only exist in a world you have listed. On a fresh install the list holds Exylia's own sandbox
worlds, which are not yours, so nothing is allowed yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit config.yml">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaPracticeBotV3/config.yml` and replace the `worlds` list with your own world
    names, exactly as the server names the folders.

    ```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPracticeBotV3/config.yml"
    worlds:
      - pvp_sandbox
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    `/bot reload` with `exyliapracticebot.admin`. No restart needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stand in that world">
    Anywhere in it. There is no arena to build and no region to define — the bot appears where you are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="If you skip this">
Every spawn is answered with "You can't spawn a bot in this world", from the command and from the
menu's spawn button alike. It is the same check in both places.
</Callout>

## Spawn a bot and fight it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    `/bot` with `exyliapracticebot.use`. The main screen is one button per decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    The **Mode** button, top middle. Left-click steps to the next mode, right-click to the previous
    one. The lore line under it says what that mode fights with. Seven to choose from; `POT_PVP` is the
    shipped default. Picking a mode applies that mode's kit as well as its combat model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a difficulty">
    The **Difficulty** button, right beside it. Left-click steps up, right-click steps down. Five rungs
    from Easy to Extreme; `NORMAL` is the shipped default. Start there — Insane and Extreme are meant
    to be walls, not fair fights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawn it">
    The green **Spawn Bot** button on the bottom bar, or `/bot spawn`. The bot appears where you are
    standing, wearing your skin and your name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fight">
    Hit it. It follows you, fights back, and heals, blocks or trades exactly as its mode and difficulty
    say it should. Nothing you do to it drops loot or gives experience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While you are fighting

- **It stops when you do.** The bot is removed when you die, when you change world, and when you get
  further than 200 blocks from it. Each one tells you which it was.
- **It comes back on its own.** After it dies it respawns two seconds later by default
  (`respawn.enabled`, `respawn.delay-seconds`).
- **Shift right-click it** to reopen the menu without typing anything — only its owner can, and only
  while `interaction.shift-click-opens-menu` is on.
- **`/bot tpto` and `/bot tpme`** close the gap in either direction, and the ender pearl in the menu
  does the same.
- **`/bot remove`** sends it away. `/bot reset` puts every setting of yours back to the server default.

## Tune it

Everything below mode and difficulty already has a working value. When you want more:

- The **armour column** down the left of the main menu: left-click a piece for the next tier, right-
  click for its enchantment.
- **Main hand**, **off hand** and **potions** down the right.
- **Fight back** turns it into a moving punching bag; **Follow you** makes it hold its ground.
- **Fine tuning** opens the second screen — damage, reach, attack and movement speed, health, totems
  and the sandbox toggles. Every slider is clamped to the bounds in the config's `limits` block.

Your settings are yours: they are stored against your UUID and are waiting for you the next time you
log in, on this server or on any server sharing the same database.
