## Installing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop in the jars">
    `ExyliaHitEffect.jar`, plus `ExyliaLib` and `packetevents`, into `plugins/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the server">
    It writes `config.yml`, `effects.yml` and `menus/main.yml`, and creates its table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant the permissions">
    See [Commands and permissions](/docs/exyliahiteffect/commands).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `config.yml` | Which victims count, the default visibility, and the visibility mode names. |
| `effects.yml` | The 9 categories and 80 effects. |
| `menus/main.yml` | The menu layout, editable like any ExyliaLib menu. |
| `database.yml` | Where selections are stored. H2 by default. |

Selections live in the `hiteffect_players` table: the chosen effect and the visibility mode, per
player.

## config.yml

```yaml
placeholders:
  none: "None"

effects:
  all-mobs: false
  allowed-mobs:
    - PLAYER
    - MANNEQUIN
    - ZOMBIE

particles:
  default-visibility: "ALL"
  name-all: "All"
  name-none: "None"
  name-self-only: "Self Only"
  name-others-only: "Others Only"
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `placeholders.none` | `None` | What the placeholders show when nothing is chosen. |
| `effects.all-mobs` | `false` | `true` plays on any living victim. `false` limits it to the list. |
| `effects.allowed-mobs` | `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN`, `ZOMBIE` | Entity types that trigger it when hit. |
| `particles.default-visibility` | `ALL` | What a player starts with. |
| `particles.name-*` | see above | How each mode is named in menus and placeholders. |

<Callout type="warning" title="all-mobs is heavier here than in a kill plugin">
A hit effect fires on every swing that lands, not once per death. With `all-mobs: true` a player
tunnelling through a mob farm plays a full sequence per hit. Keep the list limited unless you know
that is what you want.
</Callout>

## Updating

Replace the jar and restart. `/hiteffect reload` re-reads this plugin's own files.
