---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaHitEffect is and what it ships with."
---

ExyliaHitEffect plays a particle effect on whoever you hit, at the moment you hit them. Players pick
theirs from a menu, and permissions decide which ones they may pick.

**80 effects across 9 categories** ship in `effects.yml`: impact, elemental, arcane, nature, premium,
sculk, breeze, spectral and toxic.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/hiteffect` shows the categories, then the effects inside one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pick one">
    The choice is stored in the database and survives a restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They hit something">
    The effect plays on the victim.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The trigger is any damage dealt by a player to a living entity. Which victims count is configurable:
everything, or only the entity types you list.

<Callout type="note" title="It plays a block up">
The effect is anchored one block above the victim's feet, so it lands on the body rather than in the
ground — which matters far more for a hit effect than for a kill effect, since the victim is standing
up when it fires.
</Callout>

## What each effect is

An effect is a **sequence** — shapes, particles, sounds and pauses played in order:

```yaml
effects:
  - '[BURST] CRIT;radius:1.0;count:30'
  - '[SOUND] ENTITY_PLAYER_ATTACK_CRIT;1.0;1.4'
  - '[DELAY] 0.1'
  - '[PARTICLE] CRIT;count:20;offset:0.3,0.3,0.3'
```

Everything is editable and you can write your own. The full syntax is in
[Effects](/docs/exyliahiteffect/effects).

## Visibility

Every player chooses what they see, and the choice is theirs alone:

| Mode | What they see |
|---|---|
| `ALL` | Every effect. |
| `NONE` | None at all. |
| `SELF_ONLY` | Only their own. |
| `OTHERS_ONLY` | Only other people's. |

This matters more here than in a kill plugin: a hit effect fires on every swing, so a player who
finds them distracting in a fight can turn them off without affecting anybody else.
