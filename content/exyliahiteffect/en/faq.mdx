## The plugin does not start

`ExyliaLib` and `packetevents` are both required. Without either, the server disables it by name.

## The menu will not open

That needs `exyliahiteffect.command.hiteffect`. It is separate from owning any effect.

Up to version 1.0.8 this node was spelled `exyliahiteffect.command.killeffect`, inherited from the
sibling plugin. If you granted the old spelling, switch it — the old one no longer does anything.

## Nothing plays when a player hits

1. **Is the victim allowed?** `effects.all-mobs` is `false` by default, so only `PLAYER`,
   `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count.
2. **Do they own the effect?** A selection they no longer have permission for is cleared, not played.
3. **Is anyone set to see it?** A player on `NONE` sees nothing, including their own.

## Effects overlap into a mess during a fight

That is the nature of a per-hit trigger, and it is why the shipped effects are short. An effect with
`[DELAY]` lines will still be drawing when the next hit lands. Cut the delays, lower `count:`, or use
`ticks:1` so a shape is drawn in one frame instead of over time.

## The effect appears at the victim's feet

It should not — it is anchored a block up on purpose. If it looks low, check the `y:` on the shape
lines in your own effect, which is applied on top of that block.

## Can I use kill effects here?

The syntax is identical, so a sequence copies across. Whether it *reads* well is another question: a
four-second spiral designed for a death fires on every swing here.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission for it went away. The plugin clears a selection it can no longer justify rather than
playing something the player is not entitled to.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
