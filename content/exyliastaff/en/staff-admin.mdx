---
title: "Staff admin"
description: "The panel that investigates the team: who is on duty, the team board, the audit of every command, flags, notes, coverage and the module switches."
---

`/staffadmin`, or `/sadmin`, with `exyliastaff.admin.panel`, opens a hub. Everything behind it is
read from what the other modules recorded, so a module switched off still shows its history. The
one thing the panel changes is a staff member's file: notes and strikes.

Module id `staffadmin`, switch `modules.staff-admin`, files under `modules/staffadmin/`.

## The hub

| Tile | Opens |
|---|---|
| Staff online | Every open session on the network, then the staff online here without one. Left click a row for the profile, right click to teleport, shift left to follow. |
| Team | The team board. |
| Audit | Every recorded row in the window, network wide. |
| Flagged | Only the flagged ones. |
| Investigate | Asks for a name and opens that member's file. |
| Coverage | Who is on duty when, by weekday and three-hour block. |
| Modules | The sixteen switches. |
| Window | Cycles the period the panel looks at: 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. |

`/staffadmin online`, `team`, `audit`, `coverage` and `modules` skip the click, and
`/staffadmin <player>` opens a file directly.

## The audit

Three kinds of rows land in `staff_audit`:

- **Actions** the modules announce: a freeze, an inspection, a resolved report, a punishment.
- **Commands** run by anyone holding `exyliastaff.staff`, with the full line, when `log-commands` is
  on and the command is not in `ignored-commands`.
- **Items** dropped, picked up or taken out of a container by somebody in staff mode, when
  `log-items` is on.

Rows older than `keep-days` are deleted once a day. The feed reads the latest `feed-limit` rows.

## Flags

A command row is flagged by its first word. A flag is not an accusation; it is a line worth a look.

| Flag | When | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| `self_give` | A `give-commands` command whose item went to the sender, or to `@s`, or to no player name | 25 |
| `give` | Any other `give-commands` command | 8 |
| `creative` | A `gamemode-commands` command that sets creative: `gmc`, or an argument of `creative`, `c` or `1` | 10 |
| `revoke` | A `revoke-commands` command | 12 |
| `item` | An item row | 6 |
| `sensitive` | A `sensitive-commands` command | 3 |

The weights add up to a member's **risk** over the window.

## The team board

One row per staff member with anything recorded in the window, sorted by active time; the order
button cycles to risk, response time, punishments and quota. Each row shows active and total time
with the AFK share, the quota reached against `quota-hours-week`, reports and helpops handled with the
average response time, punishments with their average severity from `punishment-severity`, and how
many punishments they revoked. A member with a risk above zero is marked.

## A member's file

Status, last time on duty, first seen; time on duty, work done, punishments issued; a **Review** tile
with the flag counts, the **Audit** of their rows, **Notes**, **Inventory** if they are online and the
inspect module runs, **Follow** and **Teleport**.

**Follow** puts you in spectator view behind them, which needs the staff mode module and you in
staff mode; if they are on another server it teleports you there instead.

**Notes** and **strikes** are written from a chat prompt and kept with the author's name and the
date; shift right click removes one. Both need `exyliastaff.admin.notes`. Notes are never purged.

## Coverage

Seven weekday rows, eight three-hour blocks each, and in each block the average number of staff on
duty over the window, drawn as a bar: green from 1.5, yellow from 0.5, red below. The **Thinnest
cover** tile names the weakest block of the week. Times are the server's zone.

## Modules

One entry per module, enabled, disabled or failed. Clicking switches it, with `exyliastaff.admin.modules`,
and re-registers the commands so the server matches the module set. The panel's own module cannot
be switched off from here, and a failed module needs a restart.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/staffadmin/config.yml"
keep-days: 90
log-commands: true
log-items: true
quota-hours-week: 10
default-days: 7
feed-limit: 500
ignored-commands:
  - staffadmin
  - es
  - exyliastaff
  - msg
  - r
  - tell
  - helpop
  - report
  - list
  - spawn
give-commands:
  - give
  - i
  - item
gamemode-commands:
  - gamemode
  - gm
  - gmc
revoke-commands:
  - unban
  - pardon
  - unmute
  - untempban
  - unpunish
  - revoke
sensitive-commands:
  - op
  - deop
  - ban
  - tempban
  - mute
  - kick
  - clear
  - setblock
  - fill
  - effect
  - enchant
flag-weights:
  self_give: 25
  give: 8
  creative: 10
  revoke: 12
  item: 6
  sensitive: 3
punishment-severity:
  WARN: 1
  MUTE: 2
  KICK: 2
  TEMPBAN: 4
  BAN: 6
```

## Messages

`modules/staffadmin/messages.yml`: `never-played`, `no-data` with `%days%`, `search-prompt`,
`note-prompt`, `note-added` with `%target%`, `note-removed`, `follow-offline`, `follow-started`,
`follow-needs-staffmode`, `module-toggled` with `%module%` and `%state%`, `module-locked`, `no-flag`,
`flag-line` with `%flag%` and `%count%`.

## Storage

`staff_audit` for the rows, `staff_notes` for the notes. The board and the file also read
`staff_log_sessions`, `staff_punishments`, `staff_reports` and `staff_helpop`.
