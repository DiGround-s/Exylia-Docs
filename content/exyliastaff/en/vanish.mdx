title: "Vanish"
description: "Packet-level invisibility, rank levels, what a vanished player cannot do, and how the state follows them across servers."

Vanish is a packet visibility rule: a vanished player is not sent to the clients that may not see
them, so there is no tab entry, no sound and no footsteps to hide. Who may see them is decided by a
permission and a level.

Module id `vanish`, switch `modules.vanish`, files under `modules/vanish/`. Needs PacketEvents.

## Who sees whom

A viewer sees a vanished player when they hold `exyliastaff.vanish.see` **and** their level is at
least the vanished player's level. The level is the highest `exyliastaff.vanish.level.<n>` node the
player holds, from `1` to `levels.max`; no node is level `0`. Equal levels see each other, so a team
of the same rank still coordinates, and an admin at level 3 is hidden from a helper at level 1 even
though the helper sees vanished players. Levels are cached for thirty seconds per player, and
`levels.enabled: false` makes everyone level 0.

The same rule decides who gets the fake join and quit lines, who sees the coloured, glowing nametag,
and who keeps other plugins' join and quit messages about a vanished player. A player always sees
themself.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/vanish [player]` | `exyliastaff.vanish` | Toggles. `/v` is an alias. |
| `/vanish on [player]`, `/vanish off [player]` | `exyliastaff.vanish` | Sets the state. |

Naming another player needs `exyliastaff.vanish.others`, and a player whose level is lower than the
target's is refused with "outranks you". When vanish is changed for somebody else, the target is told
who did it and the sender gets a confirmation; the target's own enable effect does not play. The
hotbar's dye toggles the clicking player's vanish. From the console, a player must be named.

## While vanished

| Always | Configurable |
|---|---|
| Items are not picked up | No damage taken (`invulnerable`) |
| Mobs do not target the player | No hunger lost, eating still works (`no-hunger`) |
| | Pressure plates and tripwires do not trigger (`block-physical-interaction`) |
| | No breaking or placing blocks (`silent-mode`, off by default) |

## Joins, quits and other servers

With `on-join.remember` on, an enabled vanish is a row in `staff_vanish`. Before the player is even
logged in, the row is read, waiting up to three seconds for the database, so the first tab list the
other players receive already excludes them. Their join message is nulled and, for two seconds, any
chat line from another plugin containing their name is dropped for viewers who may not see them.
Staff who may see them get the fake join line instead, and the player is told they are still vanished.

A player who lost `exyliastaff.vanish` while away joins visible, and the row is deleted. Losing it
while online is noticed within five seconds and makes them visible with a message.

On quit the row stays, so the vanish is restored on the next server. Every change is also published
on the `vanish` channel, which is how `%staff_vanish_count%` counts vanished staff on every server.

<Callout type="warning" title="Disabling the module makes everyone visible">
`/exyliastaff module disable vanish` unvanishes every vanished player online and deletes their rows,
so they also rejoin visible.
</Callout>

## With staff mode

Staff mode vanishes on enter and unvanishes on exit through this module, silently: no message and no
effect, though the hotbar item still redraws. If the vanish module is off, staff mode simply does not
vanish anyone.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/vanish/config.yml"
invulnerable: true
no-hunger: true
block-physical-interaction: true
silent-mode: false
levels:
  enabled: true
  max: 10
on-join:
  remember: true
fake-messages:
  enabled: true
  swallow-others: true
nametag:
  enabled: true
  color: GRAY
  glow: true
effects:
  enable:
    action-bar:
      text: '{success}You are now {highlight}invisible{success}.'
      duration: 3.0
    sound:
      name: ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP
      volume: 0.6
      pitch: 1.4
  disable:
    action-bar:
      text: '{warning}You are now {highlight}visible{warning}.'
      duration: 3.0
    sound:
      name: ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP
      volume: 0.6
      pitch: 0.8
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `levels.max` | `10` | Highest level number looked up. Raise it only if you hand out more. |
| `on-join.remember` | `true` | Keep vanish across relogs and server switches. |
| `fake-messages.enabled` | `true` | Send the fake join and quit lines to staff who may see the player. |
| `fake-messages.swallow-others` | `true` | Drop other plugins' join and quit lines about a vanished player. |
| `nametag.color` | `GRAY` | A named text colour. Unknown names fall back to gray. |
| `nametag.glow` | `true` | Outline vanished players through walls for staff who may see them. |

## Messages

`modules/vanish/messages.yml`: `enabled`, `disabled`, `enabled-other`, `disabled-other`,
`enabled-by` and `disabled-by` with `%staff%`, `already-enabled`, `already-disabled`,
`permission-lost`, `fake-join` and `fake-quit` with `%player%`, `restored`, `higher-level`, and the
two words `status-on` and `status-off` that the placeholder returns.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_vanish_status%` | `status-on` or `status-off` for the viewer. |
| `%staff_vanish_count%` | Vanished staff on every server. |
| `%staff_vanished%` | `true` while the viewer is vanished, from the core. |
