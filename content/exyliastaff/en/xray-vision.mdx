title: "X-ray vision"
description: "Ores outlined through the stone for a staff member: which ones, how far, how many, and when it turns itself off."
---

X-ray vision outlines the ores around a staff member so they can see what a suspect is digging
towards. Each ore is an invisible, glowing, client-only display entity shaped like the block; nothing
is spawned on the server and nobody else sees it.

Module id `xrayvision`, switch `modules.xray-vision`, files under `modules/xrayvision/`. Needs
PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/xray` | `exyliastaff.xrayvision` | Toggles. `/xrayvision` is an alias. |
| `/xray on`, `/xray off` | `exyliastaff.xrayvision` | Sets it. |
| `/xray <player>`, `/xray on <player>` | `exyliastaff.xrayvision.others` | For somebody else. They are told who did it. |

The glowstone in the hotbar toggles it.

## What is scanned

The square of chunks within `radius` of the viewer's chunk, so `3` is a 7 by 7 square, from `y-radius`
blocks below the feet to as many above, clamped to the world's height. Only loaded chunks are read;
the scan takes a chunk snapshot on the region thread and searches it on a worker. Moving into a new
chunk drops the outlines of chunks that left the range and scans the ones that entered; a world
change rescans everything. With `refresh-seconds` at `0` nothing is rescanned until the viewer changes
chunk, so an ore mined in the meantime keeps its outline until then.

`max-blocks-per-player` is a budget over every outline the viewer has: once it is spent, further
chunks add nothing.

## When it turns off

Leaving staff mode turns x-ray off and remembers it off. Losing the permission does the same, within
five seconds. A quit or a module disable turns it off without forgetting it, so it comes back on the
next session with "X-ray is still on from your last session".

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/xrayvision/config.yml"
radius: 3
y-radius: 24
refresh-seconds: 0
max-blocks-per-player: 2000
outlines:
  DIAMOND_ORE: '#5DE5E5'
  DEEPSLATE_DIAMOND_ORE: '#5DE5E5'
  EMERALD_ORE: '#3FE05B'
  DEEPSLATE_EMERALD_ORE: '#3FE05B'
  ANCIENT_DEBRIS: '#FF5555'
  GOLD_ORE: '#FFD24A'
  DEEPSLATE_GOLD_ORE: '#FFD24A'
  IRON_ORE: '#E8E8E8'
  DEEPSLATE_IRON_ORE: '#E8E8E8'
```

`outlines` maps a block material to its colour, as `#RRGGBB` or a colour name such as `AQUA`. Add or
remove entries freely; an entry that is not a block or not a colour is dropped silently.

## Messages

`modules/xrayvision/messages.yml`: `enabled`, `disabled`, `enabled-other`, `disabled-other`,
`enabled-by` and `disabled-by`, `permission-lost`, `restored`, `status-on`, `status-off`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_xrayvision_status%` | `status-on` or `status-off` for the viewer. |
| `%staff_xrayvision_active%` | `true` or `false`. |
