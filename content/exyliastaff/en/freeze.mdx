---
title: "Freeze"
description: "The client-side pin, what a frozen player sees and cannot do, the isolated chat, the sidebars, and the alert when they log out."
---

Freezing a player pins them in place on the client: every position packet is dropped and answered
with a teleport back, so there is no rubber-banding fight with a movement plugin. They are blinded,
their armour is taken off and replaced with a block of ice on the head, they glow, and the only chat
they read is staff talking to them.

Module id `freeze`, switch `modules.freeze`, files under `modules/freeze/`. Needs PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/freeze <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | Freezes. `/ss` is an alias. |
| `/unfreeze <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | Releases. `/unss` is an alias. |
| `/freezetoggle <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | One or the other. `/sstoggle` is an alias. |

The console can freeze too. The packed ice in the hotbar toggles the freeze of the player you look
at, within five blocks. A player cannot freeze themself, and a target holding
`exyliastaff.freeze.bypass` cannot be frozen.

## What the frozen player gets

- The `frozen` lines, with the staff member's name, and then a reminder every `reminder-seconds`:
  a title, an action bar and a sound.
- The `frozen` sidebar: who froze them and for how long.
- Glow, the potion effects in `look.effects`, and the helmet, after the armour is saved to a snapshot.
  The armour comes back on release, and after a crash on the next join.

## What they cannot do

| Always | Configurable |
|---|---|
| Move | Take or deal damage (`block-damage`) |
| Break, place, drop, pick up items | Open any inventory (`block-inventories`) |
| Run commands outside `allowed-commands` | Be teleported, except by a plugin (`block-teleports`) |

Plugin teleports pass on purpose: a staff `/tphere` on a frozen player works. The "you cannot do that"
line is sent at most once every two seconds.

## The chat

The plugin registers one chat rule with the library. A frozen player only hears players with
`exyliastaff.freeze`, and only they hear the frozen player; a staff member who is holding somebody
frozen also stops hearing everyone else. Nothing is cancelled and nothing is re-sent: the chat plugin's
format survives, and the frozen player's lines get `chat-prefix` in front. Global chat bypasses this
rule like every other.

## Logging out

On quit the freeze is kept in the database, so the player is frozen again the moment they rejoin,
on any server, and staff are told they came back. If `alert-quit` is on, every staff member with
`exyliastaff.freeze` on every server is told they logged out, and `quit-commands` run with `%player%`:

```yaml
quit-commands:
  - 'console-proxy:ban %player% Logged out while frozen'
```

A `/stop` does none of this: the freeze is simply resumed after the restart.

## Across servers

Every freeze and release is published on the `freeze` channel. Staff on every server get the
`alert-frozen` and `alert-unfrozen` lines with the server name, and a server that has the player
online applies or releases the freeze when another one says so.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/freeze/config.yml"
reminder-seconds: 5
reminder:
  title:
    text: '{error}&lFROZEN'
    subtitle: '{letters}Do not disconnect'
    stay: 3
    fade-out: 1
  action-bar:
    text: '{warning}➥ Wait for staff instructions'
    duration: 4
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS
    pitch: 0.6
look:
  strip-armor: true
  helmet: PACKED_ICE
  helmet-name: '{error}&lFROZEN'
  glow: true
  effects:
    - BLINDNESS
    - DARKNESS
block-damage: true
block-inventories: true
allowed-commands:
  - msg
  - r
  - reply
  - tell
  - w
block-teleports: true
alert-quit: true
quit-commands: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reminder-seconds` | `5` | Seconds between reminders. |
| `look.strip-armor` | `true` | Take the armour off, give it back on release. |
| `look.helmet` | `PACKED_ICE` | Worn on the head. Empty for none. |
| `look.effects` | blindness, darkness | Potion effects held for the whole freeze. Unknown names are skipped. |
| `allowed-commands` | `msg`, `r`, `reply`, `tell`, `w` | Commands a frozen player may still run, matched on the bare label. |
| `quit-commands` | `[]` | Run on logout. `console-proxy:` needs the library's proxy bridge. |

## Messages

`modules/freeze/messages.yml`: `frozen` and `unfrozen` are lists of lines with `%staff%`; `froze`,
`unfroze`, `already-frozen`, `not-frozen` and `bypass` with `%target%`; `self`, `no-target`;
the four alerts `alert-frozen`, `alert-unfrozen`, `alert-quit` and `alert-rejoin` with `%staff%`,
`%target%` and `%server%`; `chat-prefix`; `blocked-command` and `blocked-action`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_freeze_by%` | Who froze the viewer. Empty otherwise. |
| `%staff_freeze_time%` | How long, as a clock: the viewer's own freeze, or the one they are holding. |
| `%staff_freeze_target%` | Who the viewer has frozen on this server. |
| `%staff_frozen%` | `true` while frozen, from the core. |
