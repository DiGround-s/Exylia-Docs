## Two nodes to begin

Every module asks the same question first: does this player hold `exyliastaff.staff`? That node is
what makes somebody staff: it lets them see vanished players when they also hold `exyliastaff.vanish.see`,
receive alerts, be listed in `/stafflist`, and is required together with `exyliastaff.staffmode` to
enter staff mode. Losing it while online ends the session within five seconds.

A helper group that can do a shift needs, at least:

```text
exyliastaff.staff
exyliastaff.staffmode
exyliastaff.vanish
exyliastaff.vanish.see
exyliastaff.freeze
exyliastaff.inspect
exyliastaff.teleport
exyliastaff.reports.manage
exyliastaff.helpop.manage
```

The full list, module by module, is on the [Permissions](/docs/exyliastaff/permissions) page.

## A first shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter staff mode">
    `/staff`. Your inventory is saved, the hotbar of tools appears, you are set to adventure mode,
    flying and invulnerable, and you vanish. The staff sidebar shows the session time, your vanish
    state and the open reports and help requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the hotbar">
    The clock in the first slot: left click jumps to the block you look at, right click phases
    through the wall in front of you. The dye toggles vanish, the music disc visits a random player,
    the book inspects the player you look at, the axe opens the punishment menu on them and the ice
    freezes them. Open your inventory: the barrier leaves staff mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a report">
    A report arrives as a chat alert on every server with an **Open** button. `/reports` lists them by
    priority; left click a row to see everything open against that player, right click resolves,
    shift right click dismisses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave staff mode">
    `/staff` again, or the barrier. Your inventory, armour, game mode, flight, health and hunger come
    back, you are teleported to where you entered, and vanish is turned off if you were visible before.
    Staff chat, x-ray and mining alerts are switched off with the session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Nothing you pick up goes into your inventory">
While in staff mode, items on the ground stay on the ground and every slot is locked. Your own items
are not shown; the hotbar is an overlay drawn on the client. The four leather pieces are the one
exception: they are really worn, so other staff see the uniform.
</Callout>

## What to check afterwards

- `/stafflog` opens your sessions of the last seven days: duration, AFK time, server and the actions
  counted in each one.
- `/staffadmin`, with `exyliastaff.admin.panel`, opens the hub: who is on duty, the team board, the
  audit feed, the flagged rows, coverage by weekday and the module switches.
- `/exyliastaff modules` from the console lists the sixteen modules and their state.

## The command gate

`config.yml` ships a list of commands that a staff member may only run while in staff mode: `tp`,
`gamemode`, `fly`, `give`, `vanish`, `freeze`, `xray` and their aliases among them. Out of staff mode
the command is refused with "Enable staff mode before using /tp" and hidden from tab completion. The
point is a paper trail, not a lock: every use then happens inside a logged session. Players with
`exyliastaff.gate.bypass` are never gated. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#command-gate).
