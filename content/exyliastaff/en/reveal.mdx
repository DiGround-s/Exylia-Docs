---
title: "Reveal"
description: "Staff can see the players an invisibility potion hides, drawn on the client only."
---

An invisibility potion hides a player from everyone, staff included. Reveal gives a staff member
back the thing the potion took: they see who is standing there, and nobody else's screen changes.

Module id `reveal`, switch `modules.reveal`, files under `modules/reveal/`. Needs PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/reveal` | `exyliastaff.reveal` | Toggles. `/seeinvis` and `/seeinvisible` are aliases. |

## Styles

`style` in `modules/reveal/config.yml` picks how a hidden player is drawn:

| Style | What you see |
|---|---|
| `OUTLINE` | They stay invisible and are outlined through walls. The default: it reads faster in a cave. |
| `SOLID` | They are drawn whole, as if the potion were not there. |

Either way this is a packet the staff member's client receives. The potion is still running, the
player still has it, and every other player still sees nothing.

## Where it fits

Vanish hides a staff member from players; reveal shows a staff member a player who is hiding. They
are opposite directions of the same question, and neither one touches the other: revealing does not
break a vanish, and a vanished staff member can reveal.
