---
title: "Helpop"
description: "A player asks, staff on every server are alerted, one claims it and the two talk through the request until it is closed."
---

`/helpop <message>` opens a request. Every staff member on every server gets an alert with a **Claim**
button; the one who claims it answers with `/hs reply`, and from then on whatever the player types
into `/helpop` goes to that staff member, wherever they are. The request closes when staff close it,
or on its own if nobody claimed it in time.

Module id `helpop`, switch `modules.helpop`, files under `modules/helpop/`.

## For players

`/helpop <message>` with `exyliastaff.helpop`. A player has one live request at a time: a second
`/helpop` while it is still unclaimed answers "You already have request #12 waiting", and there is a
`cooldown-seconds` wait between two requests. Once it is claimed, `/helpop <message>` is the reply
channel, with no cooldown, and the player is told if their handler is not online right now.

## For staff

Everything below needs `exyliastaff.helpop.manage`. `/helpop-staff` is the long form of `/hs`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/hs` | Opens the list. The turtle helmet in the hotbar does too. |
| `/hs list` | Prints the open and claimed requests, oldest first. Also from the console. |
| `/hs claim <id>` | Claims it. The player is told who is on it. |
| `/hs reply <id> <message>` | Answers. An unclaimed request is claimed by replying; one claimed by somebody else refuses. |
| `/hs close <id> [reason]` | Closes it, with `default-close-reason` when none is given. Any staff member can close any request. |

In the list, left click claims, right click teleports to the player, following them to their server,
shift right click closes.

## Timeout

Every thirty seconds, requests that are still unclaimed after `timeout-minutes` are closed with
`timeout-reason`, and the player is told nobody was available. A claimed request never times out.
`0` disables the timeout.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/helpop/config.yml"
cooldown-seconds: 30
timeout-minutes: 15
alert:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL
    pitch: 1.2
```

A cooldown under five minutes is not kept across a restart. `alert` is a library effect played for staff on every
server; a title or an action bar can be added to it.

## Messages

`modules/helpop/messages.yml`: `sent` and `already-open` with `%id%`; `cooldown` with `%time%`;
`alert-header`, `alert-line` and `alert-actions` with `%id%`, `%player%`, `%server%` and
`%message%`, the last one a clickable row you may empty; `list-header`, `list-line`, `list-empty`;
`not-found`, `already-claimed`; `claimed`, `claimed-notice`; `transcript-staff` and
`transcript-player`; `handler-offline`; `closed`, `closed-notice`, `timed-out`;
`default-close-reason`, `timeout-reason`; `player-offline`, `player-not-here`, `teleported`,
`following`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_helpop_open%` | Unclaimed requests on the network. |
| `%staff_helpop_claimed_by_me%` | Live requests the viewer has claimed. |
