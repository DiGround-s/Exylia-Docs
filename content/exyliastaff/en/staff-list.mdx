---
title: "Staff list"
description: "Who is online across the network, grouped by server, with vanish and staff-mode markers for those who may see them."
---

`/stafflist`, or `/slist`, with `exyliastaff.stafflist`, prints every online player holding
`exyliastaff.staff` on every server, one block per server in alphabetical order. The head in the
hotbar runs it, and so can the console.

Module id `stafflist`, switch `modules.staff-list`, files under `modules/stafflist/`.

## Presence

Being listed means being online with `exyliastaff.staff`. Nothing else: AFK is not tracked here.
Each server publishes its list on the `presence` channel when a staff member joins or quits and every
`heartbeat-seconds`; a server not heard from for `expire-seconds` is dropped. A module disable sends
an empty list so the others drop the server at once.

## What a viewer sees

A vanished member is only shown to a viewer who holds `exyliastaff.staff` and whose vanish level is
at least the member's; the console sees everyone. Staff viewers get the markers, `[V]` for vanished
and `[S]` for staff mode, after the name; other viewers get plain names and no vanished members at
all. The header counts what the viewer can see.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/stafflist/config.yml"
heartbeat-seconds: 30
expire-seconds: 90
```

## Messages

`modules/stafflist/messages.yml`: `header` with `%total%`, `server` with `%server%`, `entry` with
`%player%` and `%markers%`, `vanished-marker`, `staff-mode-marker`, `empty`, `footer`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_stafflist_total%` | Staff online on every server, vanished included. |
| `%staff_online%` | Players with `exyliastaff.staff` on this server, from the core. |
