title: "Staff log"
description: "Sessions of staff mode with their AFK time and their counted actions, per staff member, with a ranking and a summary."
---

Every stretch of staff mode is a session: when it started, when it ended, on which server, how much
of it was AFK, and how many of each action happened in it. `/stafflog` shows them; the
[admin panel](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-admin) builds its team board from them.

Module id `stafflog`, switch `modules.staff-log`, files under `modules/stafflog/`.

## Sessions

A session opens when a player enters staff mode, or when the module enables while somebody already
is, and closes when they leave it, quit, or the module disables. A quit closes the row and the next
join opens a new one, so rows are per connection and the summaries add them up.

## AFK

Time counts as AFK once the player has been idle for `afk-after-seconds`: that first stretch of an
idle spell is free, everything after it is AFK. Moving to another block, clicking, chatting, running
a command and every counted action reset the idle clock. The clock ticks every thirty seconds.

## Actions

Modules announce what a staff member did and the log counts it while their session is open on this
server: `freeze`, `unfreeze`, `inspect`, `enderchest`, `container`, `teleport`, `report_resolve`,
`report_dismiss`, `helpop_reply`, `helpop_close`, `punish`, `mining_reset` and `xray_toggle`.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/stafflog [player] [days]` | `exyliastaff.stafflog` | Sessions of the last `days`, default 7: one clock per session with duration, AFK, server and actions. |
| `/stafflog summary [player]` | `exyliastaff.stafflog` | Totals over 7 and over 30 days. |
| `/stafflog top [days]` | `exyliastaff.stafflog.others` | Every staff member with a session in the window, ranked by active time. |

Naming somebody else needs `exyliastaff.stafflog.others`. `/stafflog 30` reads the number as days
for your own log. The written book in the hotbar opens yours.

The ranking shows, per member, active time, total time, AFK time and its percentage, sessions, and
actions with an hourly rate. Idle time does not count as working.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/stafflog/config.yml"
afk-after-seconds: 300
keep-days: 90
```

Sessions older than `keep-days` are deleted once a day; `0` keeps everything.

## Messages

`modules/stafflog/messages.yml`: `no-sessions` with `%target%` and `%days%`, `never-played`,
`action-line` with `%action%` and `%count%`, `no-actions`, `no-staff` with `%days%`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_stafflog_today%` | Minutes in staff mode today, live session included. |
| `%staff_stafflog_week%` | Minutes over the last seven days. |

## Storage

`staff_log_sessions` holds one row per session, `staff_log_daily` one total per player and day.
Both need the staff mode module to have anything to record.
