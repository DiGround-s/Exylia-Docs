The mining module watches how players find ore. It does not care how much diamond somebody mines;
it cares whether they walk straight at buried veins in the dark, seconds apart, through eight blocks
of stone. Each of those is a factor, the factors add up to a score from 0 to 100, the score decays,
and staff are alerted when it crosses a level.

Module id `mining`, switch `modules.mining`, files under `modules/mining/`.

## What counts as evidence

Only ores of a family marked `valuable` feed the factors; the rest are counted like stone. Of those,
only the **first block of a vein** counts: the vein is flood-filled through face neighbours up to
`vein.max-size` blocks and remembered for `vein.remember-minutes`, so mining the rest of it is free.
And only a **buried** vein counts, one with at most `ore-exposure.partial-faces` faces open to air:
an ore on a cave wall is what a torch finds.

A block a player placed is remembered for `placed-blocks.remember-minutes` and ignored when broken.
Creative breaks are ignored, and so is a break another plugin cancelled.

## The factors

All of them read the last `window-minutes` of breaks. Each adds its points when it triggers; the sum
is clamped to 100.

| Factor | Triggers when | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 4, 8 or 13 buried veins in the window | 15, 30, 50 |
| Valuable ratio | More than 80 %, or 90 %, of broken blocks were buried valuable ore | 20, 35 |
| Straight line | The last 5 buried veins lie within 2 blocks of one line | 25 |
| Ambient light | 80 % of buried veins broken in darkness, or in dim light | 25, 12 |
| Deep mining | Below Y 16 and more than 60 % of the last 20 breaks were buried valuable ore | 15 |
| Discovery timing | 70 % of the gaps between veins were under 5 seconds; half of that for moderate | 25, 10 |
| Ore exposure | 70 % of fresh veins fully hidden, or partially | 25, 12 |
| Approach cost | 70 % of the gaps between veins were 8 blocks or fewer; half of that for moderate | 22, 10 |
| Beeline | 60 % of veins were walked at directly, over the last 20 seconds and from 8 blocks or more | 25 |

`factors.min-veins` is how many buried veins the ratio factors need before saying anything: one lucky
diamond in the dark is not a pattern. Every factor has `enabled` and its own thresholds.

## Score and levels

The score never drops when a new assessment is lower; it halves every `decay-half-life-minutes`
instead, so a player who stops giving reasons fades back to normal. The levels are thresholds on it:

| Level | Score |
|---|---|
| `warning` | 16 |
| `suspicious` | 26 |
| `very-suspicious` | 51 |
| `critical` | 76 |

## Alerts

An alert goes out when a player climbs to a higher level than the last one announced, and again
every `alerts.interval-seconds` while they stay at suspicious or above. A score wobbling on a
threshold does not alert on every block, and a warning is never repeated on the timer.

Alerts are published on the `mining` channel and delivered on every server to each staff member who
holds `exyliastaff.mining.alerts` **and** has alerts on, with the effect of that level. `/mining`
toggles them, `/mining on` and `/mining off` set them; the pickaxe in the hotbar right click does the
same. Leaving staff mode turns alerts off.

## Looking at a player

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/mining top` | `exyliastaff.mining.view` | The 45 highest scores, decayed to now, with level and top ores. From the console, the top ten as text. |
| `/mining view <player>` | `exyliastaff.mining.view` | That player's record, online or not: level, score, one item per ore mined. Teleport, back and reset buttons. |
| `/mining reset <player>` | `exyliastaff.mining.reset` | Wipes the record. Also from the console. |

`/notifications` and `/xraynotify` are aliases of `/mining`. The pickaxe left click opens the list.

An ore material the server does not know is skipped with one console warning. Reloading re-reads the
ores and the thresholds; the vein and placed-block memories keep the minutes they started with until
a restart.

## Messages

`modules/mining/messages.yml`: `alerts-on`, `alerts-off`; `alert` with `%player%`, `%level%`, `%score%`
and `%server%`; `reset` with `%target%`; `top-header`, `top-line`, `top-empty`; `not-online`,
`teleported`; `ore-line` with `%ore%` and `%count%`, `ores-none`; `status-on`, `status-off`; and the
five level names under `levels`, from `normal` to `critical`.

## Placeholders

These read the viewer's own record, which is what a staff sidebar wants.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_mining_level%` | The viewer's level name. |
| `%staff_mining_score%` | The viewer's score, decayed to now. |
| `%staff_mining_alerts%` | `status-on` or `status-off`. |
| `%staff_mining_alerts_active%` | `true` while the viewer receives mining alerts, without the colour `alerts` carries. |

## Storage

`staff_mining` keeps one row per player: ore counts, the raw score with its timestamp, the level and
the last alert. Dirty rows are written every thirty seconds, on quit and before the top list. The
window, the trail and the last announced level live in memory only.
