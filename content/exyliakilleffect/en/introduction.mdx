ExyliaKillEffect plays a particle effect where a kill happened. Players pick theirs from a menu, and
which ones they may pick is decided by permissions — which is what makes it something to sell.

**144 effects across 14 categories** ship in `effects.yml`: infernal, glacial, storm, void, arcane,
celestial, cosmic, nature, mythic, shadow, ancient, wind, sakura and primal.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/killeffect` shows the categories, then the effects inside one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pick one">
    The choice is stored in the database, so it survives a restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They kill something">
    The effect plays at the victim's location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Which kills count is configurable: everything, or only the entity types you list.

## What each effect is

An effect is a **sequence** — a list of shapes, particles, sounds and pauses, played in order:

```yaml
EMBER_BURST:
  category: infernal
  name: "<gradient:#D1693F:#D1523F><bold>EMBER BURST</bold></gradient>"
  material: BLAZE_POWDER
  priority: 1
  effects:
    - '[CIRCLE] FLAME;radius:1.5;points:24;y:0.1'
    - '[SOUND] ENTITY_BLAZE_DEATH;1.5;0.8'
    - '[DELAY] 0.15'
    - '[BEAM] FLAME;height:3.5;points:24'
    - '[EXPLOSION]'
```

Everything about them is editable, and you can write your own. The full syntax is in
[Effects](/docs/exyliakilleffect/effects).

## Visibility

Every player chooses what they see, and the choice is theirs alone:

| Mode | What they see |
|---|---|
| `ALL` | Every effect, theirs and everyone else's. |
| `NONE` | None at all. |
| `SELF_ONLY` | Only their own. |
| `OTHERS_ONLY` | Only other people's. |

It is a per-observer decision, so somebody on `NONE` stops seeing particles without stopping anyone
else's.
