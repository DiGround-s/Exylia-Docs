---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaKillEffect is: 159 display-driven kill effects, worn by the player, won out of a crate or bound to a weapon."
---

ExyliaKillEffect plays a **kill effect** where a victim fell. An effect is not a puff of particles:
it is drawn with **display entities** — real items and blocks that fall, turn, slam and settle,
animated by each player's own client rather than by the server tick. Particles are used the way a film
uses smoke: for atmosphere around something solid, never as the thing itself.

**159 effects in six categories** ship in `effects.yml`, and most of them leave the victim's body on
the ground as part of the scene.

## Two ways to own an effect

| Way | Whose it is | Which server it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **The menu** | The player's: they pick one in `/killeffect` and it plays whatever they kill with. | Practice, PvP |
| **A weapon** | The weapon's: an effect token is dropped onto a weapon and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. | Survival, economy |

`behaviour.mode` decides which is read — `player`, `weapon` or `both` — and is covered in
[Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliakilleffect/modes-and-tokens).

## The categories

| Category | Icon | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| `siege` | `NETHERITE_SWORD` | Weapons, engines and the weight of things arriving. |
| `cataclysm` | `MAGMA_BLOCK` | Fire, rock and weather that has had enough. |
| `abyss` | `CRYING_OBSIDIAN` | Void, gravity and things that take something away. |
| `aurora` | `AMETHYST_CLUSTER` | Crystal, starlight and cold. |
| `verdant` | `SPORE_BLOSSOM` | Growth, petals and rot. |
| `bonk` | `ANVIL` | Jokes. Anvils, rubber ducks and a vending machine. |

Twenty effects each. The whole list is in [Effects](/docs/exyliakilleffect/effects).

## How it plays out

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/killeffect` is one screen: the category tabs down the side, that category's effects beside them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pick one, or preview it">
    Left click wears it. Right click lifts them to an empty patch of sky, plays it against nothing and
    puts them back — owning it is not required to look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They kill something">
    The effect plays where the victim fell, for everyone who is set to see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Which kills count is configurable: everything, or only the entity types you list.

## Visibility is the observer's

| Mode | What they see |
|---|---|
| `ALL` | Every effect, theirs and everybody else's. |
| `NONE` | None at all. |
| `SELF_ONLY` | Only their own kills. |
| `OTHERS_ONLY` | Only other people's. |

Somebody on `NONE` stops seeing effects without stopping anyone else's.

## What a weapon carries

Binding writes **one value** onto the weapon — `exyliakilleffect:effect`, the effect id. The lines a
player reads under a bound weapon are drawn on the way to the client and are never written to the item,
so a shop, a crate or any other plugin reading that weapon finds exactly what it had.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Folia supported. |
| Required | `packetevents` |
| Recommended | `ExyliaLib` (a soft depend: the loader installs it) |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI` |
| Database | Always. The choice, the visibility and the favourites live in one table. |
