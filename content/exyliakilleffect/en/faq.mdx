description: "The questions that come up most."

## Nothing plays when a player kills

In order:

1. **Is the victim allowed?** `effects.all-mobs` is `false` by default, so only `PLAYER`,
   `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count.
2. **Is there an effect at all?** In `mode: weapon` the menu choice is never read, and in
   `mode: player` a bound weapon is never read.
3. **Do they own it?** A menu choice they no longer have the node for is taken off instead of played.
4. **Is anyone set to see it?** A player on `NONE` sees nothing, including their own.

## The menu says it is off

`behaviour.mode` is `weapon`. On that server the menu would change nothing, so it refuses to open
rather than pretending.

## An effect I bound to a weapon vanished

The weapon carries an id. If the key in `effects.yml` was renamed, that id names nothing any more and
the effect does not play. Renaming a key is not free: it is also a different permission node.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission for it went away — a rank expiring, usually. A choice that can no longer be
justified is taken off rather than played. The node comes back, they pick it again.

## Does losing a permission break a weapon?

No. A bound weapon plays its effect for whoever holds it: the node is asked for when the token is
bound, not on every kill. That is what makes a bound weapon worth trading.

## Can players see each other's effects?

That is each observer's own choice: `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or `OTHERS_ONLY`, set with
`/killeffect visibility` or from the menu.

## Are the bodies real entities?

No. `[RAGDOLL]` and `[NPC]` draw displays: nothing can hit them, loot them or collide with them, and
they disappear when their `life` runs out.

## Does the weapon lore get written to the item?

Never. It is added to the copy that leaves the server. Any plugin reading that weapon finds the lore
it always had, and renaming an effect updates every weapon on the server at once.

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes — that is what `effects.yml` is for. Twenty-five shapes, `PIXELS` for pictures and words, thirteen other tokens and the
display parameters are in [Sequences](/docs/exyliakilleffect/sequences). A line that cannot be read is
reported on the console and skipped; the rest of the effect still plays.

## Effects lag during big fights

Displays are animated by the client, so they are cheap to move — but every line is still a packet per
viewer. Look at how many lines an effect has and at `count:` on any particle lines. `ticks:` spreads a
shape over frames instead of sending it all at once.

## Can a region turn effects off?

Yes. ExyliaLib registers a WorldGuard flag, `kill-effects`: denying it in a region stops effects
playing where somebody died inside it. On a server without WorldGuard the flag simply allows.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
