## The plugin does not start

`ExyliaLib` and `packetevents` are both required. Without either, the server disables it by name.

## Nothing plays when a player kills

Three things to check, in order:

1. **Is the victim allowed?** `effects.all-mobs` is `false` by default, so only `PLAYER`,
   `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count until you add more.
2. **Do they own the effect?** A selection they no longer have permission for is cleared instead of
   played.
3. **Is anyone set to see it?** A player on `NONE` sees nothing, including their own.

## The menu will not open

That needs `exyliakilleffect.command.killeffect`. It is separate from owning any effect.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission for it went away — a rank expiring, usually. The plugin clears a selection it can no
longer justify rather than playing something the player is not entitled to.

## I renamed an effect and everybody lost it

The id is the permission: `EMBER_BURST` is `exyliakilleffect.effect.ember_burst`. Renaming the key
renames the node. Rename in the permission plugin at the same time, or do not rename at all.

## Effects lag the server during big fights

Each kill plays a whole sequence. Look at `count:` on the particle lines and at how many lines an
effect has — the shipped ones stay around eight for that reason. `ticks:` spreads a shape over time
rather than sending it all in one frame.

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes, that is what `effects.yml` is for. The full syntax — twenty shapes and twelve other tokens — is in
[Effects](/docs/exyliakilleffect/effects).

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
