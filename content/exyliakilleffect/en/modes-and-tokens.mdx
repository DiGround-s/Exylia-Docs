description: "Where the effect a kill plays comes from, and what the token and the remover do."

## The three modes

`behaviour.mode` decides where the effect comes from:

| Mode | Where it comes from | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| `player` | Only what the player picked in `/killeffect`. | It follows the player and plays whatever they kill with. Tokens do nothing. |
| `weapon` | Only what is bound to the weapon. | It is traded, dropped and lost with the weapon. The menu refuses to open and says so. |
| `both` | The weapon's own effect, and the player's choice for a weapon carrying none. | The default. |

In `both` the **weapon wins**: a bound weapon plays its own effect, and the menu choice only covers
weapons carrying none.

```yaml title="config.yml"
behaviour:
  mode: both
  consume-token: true
  consume-remover: true
  require-permission: true
  hide-without-permission: false
```

## The token

An item that carries one effect. It is used by **dragging it onto a weapon in your own inventory**:
pick the token up with the cursor and click it onto the weapon.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weapons only | Swords, axes, tridents, maces, bows and crossbows. Anything else is ignored and the click carries on normally. |
| One at a time | A weapon that already carries an effect is refused — take the first one off with a remover. |
| Fitting | An effect declaring `weapons` is refused on a weapon it does not list. |
| Permission | With `require-permission` on, the token needs the effect's own node. |
| Consumed | `consume-token` decides whether it is spent. Off, the token is reusable. |

What is written onto the weapon is **one value**: `exyliakilleffect:effect`, the effect id. Nothing
else on the item changes.

<Callout type="note" title="A token is not the item it is drawn as">
Tokens and removers are inert: drawn as an ender pearl one cannot be thrown, drawn as food it cannot
be eaten. Whatever it looks like, it can only be dropped onto a weapon.
</Callout>

## The remover

Dragged the same way onto a bound weapon. The effect comes off and **comes back as a token** in the
inventory of whoever removed it.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| A free slot first | With a full inventory nothing happens at all: the weapon is only written once the token is known to fit. |
| Unknown effects | A weapon carrying an id the file no longer declares is refused rather than turned into nothing. |
| Consumed | `consume-remover` decides whether the remover is spent. |

## The lines under a bound weapon

```yaml title="config.yml"
weapon-lore:
  enabled: true
  lines:
    - "{secondary}Kill Effect:"
    - " {letters_black}▎ {highlight}%effect%"
```

They travel **on packets only** and are never written to the item. Consequences worth knowing:

- A shop, a crate or any plugin reading that weapon finds exactly the lore it had.
- Renaming an effect updates every weapon on the server at once.
- Turning `weapon-lore` off hides the lines without touching a single item.

## Permission, and what happens when it lapses

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| `require-permission` | Whether playing an effect needs its node at all. Commands and the API are never limited by it. |
| `hide-without-permission` | Whether an effect the player may not use is hidden in the menu instead of shown locked. |

A choice the player can no longer justify is **taken off**, not quietly skipped: it is dropped on the
next kill, on the next menu open, on join, and on `/killeffectsadmin validate`. A starred effect is
never dropped — a shortlist is what somebody is saving up for as much as what they already have.
