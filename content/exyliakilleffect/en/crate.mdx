---
title: "The crate"
description: "Rarities, virtual keys, the reels that fall, and the blocks that open them."
---

The crate is how effects are won rather than given. A key is spent, a reel falls, and what it lands
on is written to the player's own row — owned for good, whatever happens to their rank afterwards.

## Rarities

Every effect declares a `tier` in `effects.yml`, and `config.yml` declares what the tiers are:

```yaml
tiers:
  common:
    name: "Common"
    material: GRAY_DYE
    color: "{muted}"
    chance: 60.0
    priority: 1
  rare:
    name: "Rare"
    material: LIGHT_BLUE_DYE
    color: "{info}"
    chance: 25.0
    priority: 2
  epic:
    name: "Epic"
    material: PINK_DYE
    color: "{accent}"
    chance: 12.0
    priority: 3
  legendary:
    name: "Legendary"
    material: ORANGE_DYE
    color: "{highlight}"
    chance: 3.0
    priority: 4
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What menus and placeholders call it. |
| `material` | The item the rarity is drawn with. |
| `color` | The colour its name and its effects are written in. |
| `chance` | How often a crate lands on it, **relative to the other rarities**. |
| `priority` | Where it sits in menus; lower comes first. |

Nothing here is hard-coded. Delete `legendary`, invent `mythic`, rename all four: the key is the id
`effects.yml` writes as `tier`, and an effect naming a rarity that no longer exists falls back to the
first one — it loses its colour, never its existence.

<Callout type="note" title="chance is a weight, not a percentage">
The shares shown in the menu are worked out from the weights, so four rarities weighted `60/25/12/3`
and four weighted `6/2.5/1.2/0.3` behave identically. Deleting a rarity re-spreads its odds across
the rest instead of leaving a three-percent hole.
</Callout>

## Keys

Keys are not an item. They live on the player's row, so nothing can be dropped, duped, or lost to a
full inventory:

```text
/killeffectsadmin keys give <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys take <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys set <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys check <player>
```

That is the only way in, which is what makes it safe to run from console — a shop, a vote listener,
a reward plugin or a crate on another plugin all hand keys over the same way.

```text
[console] killeffectsadmin keys give %player% 1
```

## Opening

`/killeffect crate`, the button in the corner of the effects menu, or a bound block. The first screen
asks only one thing: how many at once. Picking four spends four keys and drops four reels side by
side on the next screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="How many">
    `menus/crate.yml`. Choices are drawn up to `crate.max-at-once`, capped at seven by the width of
    the screen, and spread the way the reels will be — where you click is where that reel falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reels fall">
    `menus/crate_open.yml`. One reel per crate, each running the full height of the chest, each with
    its own faces and its own finishing line `crate.stagger-seconds` after the one before it. Every
    face keeps its name and its lore the whole way down, so a reel can be read while it falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What went past stays">
    Nothing is cleared when a reel stops. What it came within one slot of stays on screen, because
    half of what a crate is worth is the legendary that went past the line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paid out as each reel lands">
    Keys are spent and prizes decided before the first frame, but nothing is handed over until a
    reel actually stops on the line. Four crates opened at once pay out one at a time, `stagger`
    seconds apart, each with its own sound and its own line in chat. Closing the window mid-fall
    costs nothing — the reels stop being drawn and the prizes still land on their own clock. Leaving
    the server does end it: a reel that never landed hands its key back instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Duplicates

Landing on something already owned hands keys back — as many as `crate.duplicate-refund` says. At the
default of `1` a duplicate is a free re-roll rather than a loss, which is what keeps a nearly complete
collection worth opening.

Under `crate.reward: ITEM` nothing is unlocked at all, so no opening is ever a duplicate and the
refund never comes into it: every key pays out a token.

## Settings

```yaml
crate:
  enabled: true
  start-keys: 0
  duplicate-refund: 1
  reward: UNLOCK
  blocks: []
  max-at-once: 4
  spin-frames: 34
  stagger-seconds: 2.0
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, the button says so instead of opening. |
| `start-keys` | `0` | Keys a player is given the first time they are seen. |
| `duplicate-refund` | `1` | Keys handed back for something already owned. |
| `reward` | `UNLOCK` | What a crate hands over. `UNLOCK` keeps the effect on the account, `ITEM` hands over only its token, `BOTH` does both. `ITEM` and `BOTH` want `behaviour.mode` on `weapon` or `both`. |
| `blocks` | `[]` | The blocks that open the crate. Bind them in game, not by hand. |
| `max-at-once` | `4` | How many crates one player may open in one go. The screen fits seven. |
| `spin-frames` | `34` | How many faces the first reel runs through before it stops. Capped at 200, about eleven seconds. |
| `stagger-seconds` | `2.0` | How long after one reel stops the next one does. `0` stops them together. |

`on-spin`, `on-win` and `on-duplicate` are full ExyliaLib effect blocks — title, action bar, boss bar,
sound, particle and firework — played on every falling tick, and then once per reel the moment it
lands: the win, or the duplicate instead when that reel stopped on something already owned.

## Crate blocks

A physical block, anywhere in the world, that opens the crate when it is clicked:

```text
/killeffectsadmin crate block add      # the block you are looking at
/killeffectsadmin crate block remove
/killeffectsadmin crate block list
/killeffectsadmin crate block clear
```

They are written to `config.yml`, not into the world, so a bound block survives a schematic paste, a
world edit and a rollback. While it is bound it cannot be broken, blown up or pushed away, and
clicking it no longer does what its material does — a crate drawn on a barrel must not open a barrel.

## What a crate hands over

Whatever comes out is written to the player's row rather than granted as a permission, so it outlives
a rank running out. `/killeffectsadmin unlocks grant|revoke|clear` is the same door for a reward
plugin that wants to hand an effect over without a crate.

<Callout type="tip" title="Permissions and unlocks are two separate ways in">
A player may use an effect they own from a crate **or** one their rank grants. Selling a rarity as a
rank is one line — `exyliakilleffect.tier.legendary` — and it is independent of anything they unboxed.
</Callout>
