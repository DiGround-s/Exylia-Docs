---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "The seven PlaceholderAPI placeholders, and what they answer when nothing is chosen."
---

Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it.

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect%` | The chosen effect's id. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_name%` | Its display name, with its colours. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_description%` | Its description, flattened to one line. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_material%` | The material it is drawn with. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_category%` | The category id. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_category_name%` | The category's display name. |
| `%exyliakilleffect_has_effect%` | `true` or `false`. |

## With nothing chosen

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliakilleffect_has_effect%` | `false` |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_description%` | Empty |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_material%` | A fallback material |
| Everything else | Whatever `placeholders.none` says in `config.yml` — `None` by default |

That is what makes them safe to drop into a scoreboard without a fallback of their own:

```yaml
lines:
  - " {muted}❙ {letters}Effect: {highlight}%exyliakilleffect_effect_name%"
```

<Callout type="tip" title="effect_material is for menus">
It is the icon of whatever they chose, which is what lets another plugin's menu draw their current
effect without knowing anything about this one.
</Callout>
