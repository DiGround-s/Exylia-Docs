## The file

`effects.yml` has two sections: the categories, and the effects that belong to them.

```yaml title="effects.yml"
categories:
  meme:
    material: JUKEBOX
    name: 'Meme'
    priority: 1

kill_effects:
  GRIDDY:
    category: meme
    tier: common
    name: "<gradient:#B8FF9E:#59A4FF><bold>HIT THE GRIDDY</bold></gradient>"
    material: NOTE_BLOCK
    description:
      - 'Two bars of the griddy, a spin, a dab, and'
      - 'then {highlight}the whole dance goes everywhere{neutral}.'
    priority: 1
    effects:
      - '[RAGDOLL] {victim};keys:0.18 up=-0.1 body=20 arms=45,0,25 legs=12 head=10 ease=out | 0.22 leg_l=48 leg_r=-16 arm_r=85,0,15 arm_l=-45,0,15 up=0.04 forward=0.15 turn=16 head=-10 body=14 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=-16 leg_r=48 arm_r=-45,0,15 arm_l=85,0,15 up=-0.1 forward=0.3 turn=-16 head=14 body=22 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=48 leg_r=-16 arm_r=85,0,15 arm_l=-45,0,15 up=0.04 forward=0.45 turn=16 head=-10 body=14 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=-16 leg_r=48 arm_r=-45,0,15 arm_l=85,0,15 up=-0.1 forward=0.3 turn=-16 head=14 body=22 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=48 leg_r=-16 arm_r=85,0,15 arm_l=-45,0,15 up=0.04 forward=0.15 turn=16 head=-10 body=14 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=-16 leg_r=48 arm_r=-45,0,15 arm_l=85,0,15 up=-0.1 forward=0 turn=-16 head=14 body=22 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=48 leg_r=-16 arm_r=85,0,15 arm_l=-45,0,15 up=0.04 forward=-0.15 turn=16 head=-10 body=14 ease=smooth | 0.22 leg_l=-16 leg_r=48 arm_r=-45,0,15 arm_l=85,0,15 up=-0.1 forward=0 turn=-16 head=14 body=22 ease=smooth | 0.14 crouch forward=0 turn=0 ease=out | 0.4 stand up=0.75 turn=~360 arms=0,0,60 legs=0,0,10 ease=out | 0.2 dab up=0 ease=in | 0.14 up=-0.08 ease=out | 0.14 up=0 ease=in | 0.4 head=40,-24,-8;intact:0.1;detail:3;light:15;then:burst;speed:2.6;up:5.5;follow:1.1'
      - '[SOUND] BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BIT;0.8;1.0'
      - '[DELAY] 0.28'
      - '[SOUND] BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT;0.7;1.3;repeat:8;every:0.22'
      - '[SOUND] BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS;0.8;0.8;repeat:4;every:0.44'
      - '[PARTICLE] NOTE;count:2;offset:0.7,0.3,0.7;speed:1.0;y:2.3;repeat:8;every:0.22'
      - '[CIRCLE] LIME_CONCRETE;as:block;radius:1.4;points:8;y:0.04;size:0.32,0.03,0.32;orbit:0.5;life:1.9;light:15;glow:{success}'
      - '[DELAY] 1.9'
      # … the sweep, the dab and the confetti burst follow
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `category` | Which tab it appears under. Must be a key declared under `categories`. |
| `tier` | Its rarity: a key declared under `tiers` in `config.yml`. Decides its colour and how often a crate lands on it. One that no longer exists falls back to the first rarity. |
| `name` | What menus and placeholders call it. Palette tokens, MiniMessage and gradients all work. |
| `material` | The item the row is drawn with. Defaults to `BARRIER`. |
| `description` | A list is one line per entry; a single string breaks on `<nl>`. |
| `priority` | Where it sits in its tab; lower comes first. Defaults to `999`. |
| `effects` | The steps that are drawn where the victim fell. |
| `weapons` | Optional. What the effect may be bound to. Omit it and it fits anything. |

A category takes `material`, `name` and `priority`.

<Callout type="note" title="Broken entries are reported, not guessed">
A missing `categories` section, an effect naming a category that is not declared, an entry with no
`effects`, and a `weapons` token that is neither an item nor a family are each written to the console
with the key that caused them. The rest of the file still loads.
</Callout>

## `weapons`

Only read while `behaviour.mode` reads weapons. It accepts item names and families:

```yaml
weapons: [ NETHERITE_SWORD, AXE ]
```

| Family | Matches |
|---|---|
| `ANY` | Anything at all. |
| `SWORD` | Every `_SWORD`. |
| `AXE` | Every `_AXE` — a pickaxe is not one. |
| `TRIDENT`, `MACE`, `BOW`, `CROSSBOW` | Just that item. |
| `MELEE` | Swords, axes, tridents and maces. |
| `RANGED` | Bows, crossbows and tridents. |

Families are matched on the item's name, so a sword added in a future Minecraft version is a sword the
day it exists. `ANY_SWORD` reads the same as `SWORD`. None of the shipped effects declares `weapons`,
so out of the box every one of them fits every weapon.

<Callout type="note" title="The effect id is the permission">
`GRIDDY` is `exyliakilleffect.effect.griddy`. Renaming the key renames the node, and everybody who
had the old one loses the effect. Case is not part of it: ids are lowercased on the way in.
</Callout>

## The body on the ground

Every one of the 100 effects is built on the `[RAGDOLL]` step: the victim's own body, every joint
kept and each part wearing the colours of their own skin, choreographed frame by frame. It dances, is
slapped, carried off or spelled into a word, and then bursts, collapses or dissolves. In 37 of them a
copy of the killer walks in and acts the death out, and in 16 the players watching nearby — or
strangers, when nobody is — join in.

Every piece is a display, not an entity anyone can hit — nothing can be looted from them and nothing
collides with them. The choreography and its parameters are in
[Sequences](/docs/exyliakilleffect/sequences).

## Writing your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the entry">
    A new key under `kill_effects`, with a `category` that exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the steps">
    A sound, a shape, a delay. The full syntax is in [Sequences](/docs/exyliakilleffect/sequences).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    `/killeffectsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at it">
    `/killeffect preview <id>` plays it against an empty sky, then puts you back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant it">
    `exyliakilleffect.effect.<id in lowercase>`, or the whole category.
  </Step>
</Steps>
