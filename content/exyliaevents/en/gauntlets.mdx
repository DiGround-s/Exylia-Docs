---
title: "Gauntlets"
description: "Several events back to back, with points per stage and a champion at the end."
---

A **gauntlet** is a series of events played one after another with the same players. Every stage hands
out points, and whoever has the most when the last one ends is the champion.

Series are put together from the menus out of events that already exist, kept in the database
(`event_gauntlets`), and need no file editing at all.

<Callout type="note" title="One gauntlet runs at a time">
A series needs the event slots and the players, so starting one while another is running is refused
with a message.
</Callout>

## Building a series

`/eventsadmin` leads to the gauntlet list: create a series, give it a name and an icon, and add stages
by picking them from the existing configurations. Stages can be reordered and removed without a
reload.

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| `id` | The key, as you typed it. |
| `displayName` | What the menu shows. Defaults to the id. |
| `iconMaterial` | The icon. Defaults to `NETHER_STAR`. |
| `stages` | The configuration ids, in play order. |

Every event type works as a stage. A series needs **at least two** before it can be started.

## Starting one

```text
/eventsadmin gauntlet start <series>
/eventsadmin gauntlet stop
```

`start` pulls in every online player who is free — not already in an event — and needs at least two of
them. `stop` cuts the series short along with the stage it is playing.

The series menu has a start button that does the same thing.

## The line a player builds

A player with `exyliaevents.start` can put together their own line from the menu: every click adds one
of the startable events, up to **12 stages**, and START runs it. That line is **not saved** — it
belongs to that run, and the next player builds their own.

The same rules as `/events start` apply: the start cooldown (unless they have
`exyliaevents.cooldown.bypass`) and the configured main lobby.

## How a run goes

<Steps>
  <Step title="The stage starts">
    The stage's event is started and the roster is pushed into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forced start">
    After `lobby-seconds` the event starts even if its own countdown had not finished. A stage with
    fewer than two players is skipped and announced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Points">
    When it ends, winners earn `points-winner` and everybody else who played earns
    `points-participant`. The top three standings are broadcast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    After `between-stages-seconds` the next stage begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Champion">
    Once the last stage ends, whoever has the most points is crowned — several players on a tie — and
    `champion-commands` run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Whoever joins a stage's lobby is in the gauntlet from then on, so arriving late to the series does not
stop a player scoring in what is left of it.

## Configuration

```yaml title="config.yml"
gauntlet:
  points-winner: 10
  points-participant: 2
  lobby-seconds: 20
  between-stages-seconds: 8
  champion-commands: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `points-winner` | `10` | Points for winning a stage. |
| `points-participant` | `2` | Points for playing one without winning. |
| `lobby-seconds` | `20` | Lobby seconds before a stage is force-started. |
| `between-stages-seconds` | `8` | The pause between one stage and the next. |
| `champion-commands` | empty | Console commands for the champion. `%player%` and `%series%`. |

<Callout type="tip" title="The champion is rewarded separately">
Each event's normal rewards still pay out per stage. `champion-commands` is the prize for the whole
series, so that is where the big one goes.
</Callout>
