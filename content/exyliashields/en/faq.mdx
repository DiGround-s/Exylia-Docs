## The plugin does not start

`ExyliaLib` and `packetevents` are both required. Without either, the server disables it by name.

## The menu opens but a player cannot save anything

They have no slot. `exyliashields.max_slots.<n>` is what grants them, and a player with none of those
nodes gets zero — grant at least `exyliashields.max_slots.1`.

## Designs disappear from shields

That is `force_system`, on by default: a player without `exyliashields.use` has their design stripped
whenever they equip a shield. Grant the node, or turn the system off in `config.yml`.

## Half the patterns are locked

They are permissions: `exyliashields.pattern.<id>` and `exyliashields.color.<id>`, with `*` for all of
each. That is the palette, and it is meant to be sold.

## A player's design vanished when their shield broke

It should not — the design is stored against the player, not the item, so a replacement shield comes
back decorated on the next inventory change. If it stays plain, check `exyliashields.use` and whether
the slot is still the active one.

## Can a player wear somebody else's shield?

Not by picking the item up: the design travels with the player, not the shield. What they can do is
import the design from the library by id, which copies it into one of their own slots.

## An imported design looks wrong

An import is drawn from the **importer's** permissions. A design built with a pattern they do not own
cannot be used to bypass the palette.

## How deep can a design go?

`max_layers` in `shields.yml`, six by default. Every layer is a banner pattern the client renders on
every shield in view, so raising it has a real cost.

## Are there placeholders?

No. This plugin registers none — what it has instead is [an API](/docs/exyliashields/api) for reading
the active design, the slot counts and the permission checks.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
