title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaShields is: a layered shield design editor players use from a menu."
---

ExyliaShields is a shield design editor. A player stacks patterns and colours into a design, saves it
in a numbered slot, and whatever is in the active slot is applied to the shield they carry — no
loom, no banner, no crafting.

**41 patterns and 16 colours**, layered up to six deep by default.

## The four screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Slots** | The player's saved designs. Create, select, reset, delete. `/shields` opens here. |
| **Design editor** | Building one design: add a layer, dye it, remove it, preview it. |
| **Colour picker** | Choosing the colour for the layer being added. |
| **Community browser** | The most-used designs on the server, importable by id. |

## Slots

A design lives in a slot, and how many slots a player gets is a permission —
`exyliashields.max_slots.<n>`. That is the shape of the product: the free rank gets one, a paid rank
gets five, and the higher node wins so an upgrade never revokes the lower one.

The **active** slot is what gets applied. Switching slots is how a player changes shields without
rebuilding anything.

## Layers

A design is a stack of layers, each a pattern plus a colour, on top of a base. `max_layers` in
`shields.yml` caps the stack — six by default.

<Callout type="note" title="Patterns and colours are permissions too">
`exyliashields.pattern.<id>` and `exyliashields.color.<id>`, with `*` for all of each. A design is
built from what the player is allowed to use, so a rank can be sold as a palette rather than as a
number of slots.
</Callout>

## When the design is applied

Whenever the shield they are holding changes: swapping hands, clicking in the inventory, scrolling the
hotbar, or any inventory slot changing. The design is stored against the player, not the item — a
shield that breaks and is replaced comes back decorated.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Folia supported. |
| Required | `ExyliaLib`, `packetevents` |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI` |

<Callout type="warning" title="No placeholders">
Unlike the other cosmetic plugins in the ecosystem, this one registers none. What it exposes instead
is [an API](/docs/exyliashields/api) another plugin can read.
</Callout>
