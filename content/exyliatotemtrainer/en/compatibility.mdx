title: "Compatibility"
description: "How the plugin shares players with the rest of the Exylia ecosystem, what it needs from the server, and what it deliberately does not do."
---

A player belongs to one Exylia mode at a time, and ExyliaLib owns that fact. This plugin reads it
before anything starts and writes it while a player is inside.

## One player, one activity

Entering solo training or a duel takes a **claim** through ExyliaLib's
[`Sessions`](/docs/exylialib/players#one-player-one-activity), of kind `TRAINING` or `MATCH`.
ExyliaPracticeCore, ExyliaFFA, ExyliaSandBox, ExyliaEvents and anything else built on the library see
the player as taken for as long as the claim lasts, and the claim is released last on the way out,
after the inventory is back.

The question is asked in both directions, and in two halves:

| Half | What it means | What the player reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Claim** | Another plugin holds the player: an event, an FFA arena, a practice match. | "You are busy in %plugin%.", naming that plugin. |
| **Advice** | Nobody holds the player, but their plugin says they are mid-something, such as Practice's "in a party". | "Not right now: %reason%.", in that plugin's own words. |

Advice is not a veto: a plugin that must have the player is unaffected. It is what stops a duel
request from being sent to somebody halfway through a kit editor, and what stops this plugin from
pulling a party member out of a practice lobby.

This plugin offers advice of its own: while a duel request is **open on either side**, both players
are reported as "answering a duel request". Neither of them holds a claim yet, and taking one of them
away is how the other ends up waiting for an answer that can no longer come.

If another plugin later asks for a player this one holds, the library tells this plugin to let go: a
solo session ends as `CANCELLED`, a duel is forfeited.

## The opponent picker

The **Duel** screen lists everyone online except the viewer and the players they cannot see, so a
vanished staff member is not on it. Players who are free come first; a busy player stays on the list,
sinks below every free one and cannot be clicked, with the reason on the row: "Busy right now" for a
claim, or the advising plugin's own words. The list is refreshed every second while the window is
open, so somebody who just stepped into an arena drops out of reach without being reopened.

## After a crash

Entering a session saves the player's inventory before it is cleared. If the server dies between the
two, the snapshot is still there: on the player's next join it is restored, and they are sent back to
where the oldest snapshot says they were before any of this started, or to `arena.return-location`
when one is set. Nobody wakes up inside an arena holding a stack of totems.

## What the server needs

| Plugin | Role |
|---|---|
| `ExyliaLib` | Configuration, menus, database, placeholders, effects, sessions, snapshots, teleports. The Lukittu loader installs it, which is why `plugin.yml` lists it as soft. |
| `packetevents` | Declared as a hard dependency: the server does not load the plugin without it. It is what draws the visual isolation, so two duels in one arena cannot see each other. |
| `PlaceholderAPI` | Optional. Publishes the placeholders outside the plugin under two identifiers, `exyliatotemtrainer` and `exyliatotem`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/placeholders). |

<Callout type="warning" title="If the library's packet layer is unavailable">
Isolation falls back to `hidePlayer` and `showPlayer`, and the console says so once: "PacketEvents is
missing: match isolation falls back to hidePlayer". The rule is the same, only applied more coarsely.
</Callout>

Folia is supported: everything that touches a player runs on that player's thread, a match's own
bookkeeping runs at its arena, and the events the plugin fires arrive on the thread that owns the
player they are about.

## What is not here

- **No queue.** A duel is always a chosen opponent: `/totem duel <player>` or a click in the picker.
- **No rating.** Wins, losses, streaks and a win rate, nothing ranked. The leaderboards are about what
  a mode produces, and they live on [Statistics](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/stats).
- **No cross-server play.** Servers sharing a database share arenas, profiles, records and history,
  but a duel happens on the server both players are on.
