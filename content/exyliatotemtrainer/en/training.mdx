Solo training is one player, one engine, one sidebar. The engine is the same object a duel uses, so
everything on this page about hits, reactions and protections applies to duels too; what a duel adds
is on [Duels](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/duels).

## The mechanic

Every interval the engine deals a **lethal hit** that a totem in either hand absorbs. The player then
has to get the next totem into a hand before the following hit. The time between the hit and the
re-equip is the reaction, and the reaction is what gets graded.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before the hit | The hands are checked server-side. Main hand or off hand holding a totem: the hit is dealt. Neither: the player **failed**, no hit is dealt, no death happens. |
| The hit | `Player#damage` with cause `CUSTOM` and a lethal amount. The totem pops. |
| The re-equip | Swapping hands, scrolling the hotbar, clicking or dragging in the inventory: any way a totem reaches a hand. Evaluated one tick later, when the server's inventory is the one the player sees. |
| The reaction | Measured from the hit to the re-equip, in nanoseconds, then graded. See [Grading](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/grading). |
| The restock | One tick after the pop the mode puts totems back: a stocked mode tops up its count in random empty slots, a full-inventory mode refills or completes. See [Modes](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/modes). |

Ticks drive the cadence; nanoseconds measure the reaction. The interval is the speed the player picked
in ticks, unless the mode draws it from a window or speeds it up.

<Callout type="note" title="Nobody ever sees a death screen">
A player without a totem in hand is not hit at all. The engine reports a fail instead, so a session
ends with a title and a message, never with a respawn.
</Callout>

## A session, start to finish

`/totem train <mode> [ticks]`, or a mode in the **Solo** screen of `/totem` after picking a speed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checks">
    The mode has to exist and the interval has to be one of `training.ticks`. A player already
    training or duelling reads "Finish what you are doing first."; one held by another Exylia plugin
    reads "You are busy in %plugin%."; one another plugin advises against taking reads "Not right now:
    %reason%." See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/compatibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim and snapshot">
    The player is claimed for `TRAINING`, their location is remembered, their inventory is saved and
    cleared, and they are put in **adventure** mode whatever they walked in as.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Isolation">
    They join a group of their own: they see nobody and nobody sees them, and they cannot be hit or
    touched from outside. See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/compatibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arena, or right here">
    The least-loaded ready arena is picked and the player is sent to its spawn A. With no ready arena
    they train **where they stand**. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/arenas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Countdown">
    `training.countdown` seconds (`3.0` by default) with a title and an action bar. The default subtitle
    tells them to press E and swap totems from their inventory. **Punching the air ends the countdown
    at once.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="The drill">
    The totems are handed out, the held slot is set to the first one, the first hit is scheduled and
    the training sidebar appears. See [Scoreboard](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/scoreboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The chat confirms the start: "Training started: %mode% · %ticks% ticks."

## How a session ends

| Reason | When | What the player sees |
|---|---|---|
| `FAILED` | No totem in hand when a hit landed | The `fail` effect ("POPPED OUT") and "Missed a totem after %pops% pops.", then the summary |
| `COMPLETED` | A full-inventory mode without refill ran out of totems | The `training-complete` effect ("COMPLETE") and "Training complete!", then the summary |
| `LEFT` | `/totem leave`, or the leave button in the menu | The summary; the command also says "You left." |
| `DISCONNECTED` | The player quit | Nothing; the numbers are stored |
| `CANCELLED` | Another plugin took the player, or the plugin was disabled | Nothing; the numbers are stored |

Whatever the reason, the session is stored when it had **at least one pop or one fail**: the
lifetime profile moves and the record for that mode and speed is merged. See
[Statistics](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/stats). The `TrainingEndEvent` carries the same reason.

### The summary

`training.summary` in `messages.yml` is a list of lines, sent after `FAILED`, `COMPLETED` and `LEFT`.
It knows:

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%mode%` | The mode's display name |
| `%ticks%` | The interval picked |
| `%pops%` | Totems popped |
| `%fails%` | Fails, 0 or 1 in solo training |
| `%best%` | Fastest reaction, seconds with two decimals |
| `%average%` | Average reaction, seconds with two decimals |
| `%streak%` | Best streak of consecutive pops |
| `%score%` | The 0 to 100 score |
| `%duration%` | How long the session lasted |

## While inside

The same protections apply to solo training and duels.

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Damage | Everything but the engine's own hit is cancelled, including another plugin's `damage()` call with the same cause. |
| Hunger | Frozen. |
| Items | Dropping and picking up are cancelled. |
| Other players | Hitting, right-clicking or being hit by anyone outside your group is cancelled. Players inside a session are not collidable. |
| Commands | Only the labels in `match.allowed-commands` run (`totem`, `tt`, `totemtrainer`, `msg`, `r`, `tell`, `w` by default). Anything else reads "Leave with /totem leave before using that command." A `plugin:command` prefix is stripped before the check. |
| Movement | Free in solo training. **Pinned on the horizontal axes in a duel**, falling allowed. |
| Leaving by other means | A teleport the plugin did not do (an ender pearl, a chorus fruit) or a world change ends the session, or forfeits the duel. |
| A death from elsewhere | If another plugin kills the player through a path the damage guard did not see, the inventory is kept, the drops are cleared, the console warns and the player is taken out. |

## Leaving

Exit runs in a fixed order: the engine stops and the loadout is taken back, the inventory and game
mode are restored from the snapshot, the player is teleported back to where they came from (or to
`arena.return-location`), and the claim is released **last**, so no other mode can dress the player
while their inventory is still on its way back.

<Callout type="note" title="After a crash">
A snapshot left behind by a restart is restored on the player's next join, and they are sent back to
where they were before the session started. Nobody wakes up inside an arena.
</Callout>
