title: "Grading"
description: "The five grades, the thresholds per speed and per mode, the 0–100 score, and what a player sees and hears on every pop."
---

Every re-equip is timed from the hit to the moment a totem is back in a hand, and graded against
thresholds in `performance`. A session's grades and fails become a score from 0 to 100, which is what
the `rating` leaderboard ranks by, weighted by the speed it was set at. What each grade looks and sounds like is `feedback`.

## The grades

| Grade | Default threshold | Points |
|---|---|---|
| `PERFECT` | 0.25 s | 100 |
| `EXCELLENT` | 0.40 s | 85 |
| `GOOD` | 0.60 s | 70 |
| `OK` | 0.90 s | 50 |
| `SLOW` | anything slower than `ok` | 25 |

A threshold is the **slowest** reaction, in seconds, that still earns the grade, inclusive: a
reaction of exactly 0.25 s is `PERFECT`.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaTotemTrainer/config.yml"
performance:
  defaults:
    perfect: 0.25
    excellent: 0.40
    good: 0.60
    ok: 0.90
  by-ticks:
    "10":
      perfect: 0.15
      excellent: 0.25
      good: 0.35
      ok: 0.45
  by-mode: {}
  points:
    perfect: 100
    excellent: 85
    good: 70
    ok: 50
    slow: 25
  fail-penalty: 15
  full-credit-pops: 20
```

## Thresholds per speed and per mode

The thresholds a session uses are resolved once when it starts, most specific first:

1. `by-ticks.<ticks>` — the tick interval, written as a string key such as `"10"`.
2. `by-mode.<modeId>` — the mode id.
3. `defaults`.

The shipped file grades a 10-tick session harder than a 25-tick one: at half a second between hits,
0.45 s is the slowest reaction that still counts as `OK`. A mode entry is checked only when no entry
matches the speed, so a `by-ticks` block always wins over a `by-mode` block.

## The score

The score of a session, a round or a whole duel is:

1. The **mean** of the points of every pop's grade.
2. While the session has fewer pops than `full-credit-pops`, the mean is **scaled** by
   `pops / full-credit-pops`. A full session is untouched; `full-credit-pops: 0` turns the scaling off.
3. Rounded, minus `fail-penalty` per fail, clamped to 0..100. No pops at all is 0.

Five pops, all `GOOD`, no fail, with the defaults: a mean of 70, scaled by 5/20 to 17.5, rounded to
**18**. The same five pops ending in a miss: 18 − 15 = **3**. Twenty pops all `GOOD` ending in a
miss: 70 − 15 = **55**.

<Callout type="note" title="Why the scaling exists">
A mean on its own rates one lucky pop as highly as a long clean run: a single `GOOD` pop would sit at
70 above a twenty-two pop session on a leaderboard sorted by score. Scaling below `full-credit-pops`
makes a short session score like a short session.
</Callout>

In a duel, each round is scored on its own and the match score is the average of the round scores.
Pops, fails and the best reaction are summed across rounds.

## Feedback

Everything a player sees and hears is an ExyliaLib effect under `feedback`: a title, an action bar,
a sound, and optionally a boss bar, particles or a firework. Remove a channel to keep it quiet; remove
a whole section and nothing plays. The full shape of an effect block is documented in the
[library's effects page](/docs/exylialib/effects).

### Per grade

Each grade has an `effect` and a `chat` line, empty for none. Both know four placeholders:

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%grade%` | The grade name, `PERFECT` to `SLOW`. |
| `%reaction%` | The reaction in seconds, two decimals, no unit. |
| `%pops%` | Pops so far in this session or round. |
| `%streak%` | Pops in a row since the last fail. |

By default a grade shows no title, plays `ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP` at volume 0.8 and a pitch
that drops with the grade, and writes the action bar:

| Grade | Action bar | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| `perfect` | `{success}⚡ PERFECT {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s` | 1.8 |
| `excellent` | `{success_light}✦ EXCELLENT {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s` | 1.5 |
| `good` | `{info}✔ GOOD {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s` | 1.2 |
| `ok` | `{warning}• OK {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s` | 1.0 |
| `slow` | `{error}▼ SLOW {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s` | 0.7 |

Only `perfect` has a chat line by default: `%prefix% {success}Perfect pop! {highlight}%reaction%s`.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaTotemTrainer/config.yml"
feedback:
  perfect:
    effect:
      action-bar:
        text: "{success}⚡ PERFECT {muted}· {letters}%reaction%s"
        duration: 2.0
      sound:
        name: ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP
        volume: 0.8
        pitch: 1.8
        category: MASTER
    chat: "%prefix% {success}Perfect pop! {highlight}%reaction%s"
```

### The other moments

| Section | When | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `fail` | No totem in hand when a hit landed | Title `{error}&lPOPPED OUT` / `{letters}You did not swap in time`, sound `ENTITY_ITEM_BREAK` 1.0 · 0.8 |
| `countdown` | Before the first hit of a session or round | Title `{primary}&l%time%` / `{letters}Press {highlight}E {letters}and swap totems from your inventory`, action bar `{secondary}Starting in {highlight}%time%s`, sound `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT` 1.0 · 1.4. `%time%` is the seconds left. |
| `match-found` | Both duellists arrived in the arena | Title `{primary}&lMATCH FOUND` / `{letters}Teleporting…`, sound `ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP` 1.0 · 1.2 |
| `round-won` | A round went to you | Title `{success}&lROUND WON`, sound `ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP` 1.0 · 1.4 |
| `round-lost` | A round went to your opponent | Title `{error}&lROUND LOST`, sound `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS` 1.0 · 0.7 |
| `round-draw` | Both dropped the totem in the same tick | Title `{warning}&lDRAW` / `{letters}Both of you dropped it — replaying`, action bar `{warning}Round replayed`, sound `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_SNARE` 1.0 · 0.8 |
| `match-won` | The series is yours | Title `{success}&lVICTORY` / `{letters}Match won`, sound `UI_TOAST_CHALLENGE_COMPLETE` |
| `match-lost` | The series is theirs | Title `{error}&lDEFEAT` / `{letters}Match lost`, sound `ENTITY_WITHER_HURT` 0.6 · 0.8 |
| `training-complete` | A full-inventory mode without refill ran out | Title `{success}&lCOMPLETE` / `{letters}Every totem used`, sound `UI_TOAST_CHALLENGE_COMPLETE` |

Sound values are `volume · pitch`. The countdown's title and action bar count the same seconds and
stop together, whether the timer ran out or a punch cut it short.

<Callout type="tip" title="Per-grade chat lines">
The chat line accepts the same placeholders as the effect. A server that wants a quieter screen can
empty the action bars and write one chat line per grade instead; a server that wants none of it
removes the `chat` values and keeps the sounds.
</Callout>
