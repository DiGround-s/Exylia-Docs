Everything below happens in menus. The only thing typed is the arena's id.

## Creating an arena

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin menu">
    `/totemtraineradmin` (or `/ttadmin`) with `exyliatotemtrainer.admin`. The overview shows how
    many arenas exist and how many are ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Arenas → the create button. Type a short id such as `totem1`; it is lowercased and refused if it
    already exists. The arena appears enabled, with no spawns yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    On the arena screen, **Display name** asks for what players see. Colours are allowed, 64
    characters at most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set both spawns">
    Click **Spawn A**, then stand where it goes; the wizard stores your position. Do the same for
    **Spawn B**. Player A of a duel appears at spawn A and player B at spawn B; solo sessions use spawn A.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it reads ready">
    An arena is ready when it is enabled and both spawns are set in loaded worlds. The arena list
    shows every arena as ready or incomplete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Training needs no arena">
Solo training works with no arena at all: the player trains where they stand. Duels are the ones
that need a ready arena; without one the request is answered with "No arena is ready".
</Callout>

## A first solo session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    `/totem` opens the main menu. Click **Solo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a speed">
    The bottom row lists the intervals from `training.ticks`, 25 down to 5 ticks by default. The
    selected one is remembered for the next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Each mode row shows what it does and your best at the selected speed. Clicking starts the
    session: the inventory is saved and cleared, the totems are handed out, and a countdown runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Punch the air to start now">
    A swing during the countdown skips it. Then swap the next totem in before every hit, with `F`,
    the hotbar, or the inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/totem train <mode> [ticks]` does the same without the menu, and `/totem leave` ends a session.

## A first duel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an opponent">
    `/totem` → **Duel** lists everyone online, free players first; busy ones cannot be clicked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the settings">
    On the opponent's screen choose a speed and a best-of format, then click a mode. That click sends
    the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The other side accepts">
    They receive a line with clickable **Accept** and **Decline** buttons, or run `/totem accept`.
    The request expires after `match.duel-request-expiry` seconds, 30 by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the rounds">
    Both players are teleported, a countdown runs (punch the air once each to start at once), and
    the round goes to whoever keeps a totem in hand. The first to `n/2 + 1` rounds wins the series.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/totem duel <player> [mode] [ticks] [bestOf]` sends a request straight from the command line.
