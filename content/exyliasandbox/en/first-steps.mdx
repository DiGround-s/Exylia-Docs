## The nodes a player needs

Nothing is declared in `plugin.yml`. There is no `permissions:` block, so no node carries a `default:`
— an operator passes every check because operators pass every check, and everybody else starts with
nothing.

A group that can play needs, at least:

```text
exyliasandbox.command.rtp
exyliasandbox.command.rtpqueue
exyliasandbox.command.rtpqueuefast
exyliasandbox.command.leavertpqueue
exyliasandbox.command.leavesandbox
exyliasandbox.command.back
exyliasandbox.command.tpa
exyliasandbox.command.rekit
exyliasandbox.command.kitroom
exyliasandbox.command.kitcreator
exyliasandbox.command.predefinedkits
exyliasandbox.max_kits.3
```

`exyliasandbox.admin` is the only node an administrator needs: it covers `/sandbox`, every button in
every admin screen, and every action behind them. The full list is on the
[Permissions](/docs/exyliasandbox/permissions) page.

<Callout type="note" title="`max_kits` is a tier, and the highest one wins">
The plugin walks down from `exyliasandbox.max_kits.100` to `.1` and takes the first node the player
holds. A player with none gets **one** slot. Grant exactly one node per group; a player holding `.3`
and `.5` gets five.
</Callout>

## A first world

Five worlds ship seeded — `desert`, `plains`, `snowy`, `mushroom` and `badlands`, all with a 3000-block
border. They are created and pre-generated on the first boot, which is what triggers the restart. If
that is enough, skip to the next section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    `/sandbox` → **MANAGE WORLDS**. Left-click a row to configure it, right-click to teleport into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create one">
    **CREATE WORLD** asks for an id and a display name in chat, then opens the world screen. Nothing is
    written until you press **SAVE WORLD**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the terrain">
    **GENERATION** cycles the five presets — Plains, Desert, Snowy, Mushroom, Badlands. **BIOME** takes
    a biome id. **BORDER** is the diameter: left-click adds 500, right-click removes 500, shift-left
    types a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, and wait for the restart">
    **SAVE WORLD** creates the folder and queues the pre-generation. When Chunky finishes the last
    queued world, the server shuts down. Bring it back up and the world is `READY`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Importing an existing world instead is **IMPORT WORLD**: it asks for the folder name, checks it holds a
`level.dat`, and from then on the plugin never touches its terrain or deletes it.

## A first kit

A player with no kit cannot enter a sandbox at all — every entry point refuses with `queue.no-kit`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the creator">
    `/kitcreator` opens the slot grid, `%current_kits%/%max_kits%` in its title. **+ NEW KIT** takes the
    first free slot and teleports the player into the kit-creator room with an empty inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the gear">
    The kit room opens by itself a moment later, or with `/kitroom`. Click to take a copy — the shelf
    never empties. Q drops a stack, F puts it in the offhand, shift-click takes a full stack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    `/kitsave` stores the whole inventory: storage, armour and offhand. The first kit a player saves
    becomes their favourite automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or copy a template">
    **PREDEFINED KITS** in the creator, or `/predefinedkits`, lists what the admins built. Clicking one
    shows the player's own slots; clicking a slot copies the template into it, overwriting whatever was
    there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="The favourite is what a teleport pad uses">
Standing on a teleport pad requires a favourite kit and nothing else — there is no kit menu in that
flow. A player with no favourite is told so and left standing. The star in **Kit options** toggles it,
so clicking the current favourite leaves them with none.
</Callout>

## The round trip

<Steps>
  <Step title="In">
    `/rtp` opens the world list; the stack size on each row is the number of players inside it. Picking
    a world with two or more usable kits opens the kit list; with exactly one it goes straight in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arriving">
    Two ticks after the teleport the player is reset — survival, no flight, no potion effects, full
    health, food and saturation, fall distance cleared — the sidebar appears and the kit goes on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Out">
    `/leavesandbox`, or death, or a disconnect. All three restore the inventory, hide the sidebar, end
    the playtime session and release the claim. Death is cancelled outright: nothing drops, there is no
    respawn screen, and the death location becomes the `/back` point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to check afterwards

- `/sandbox` → **MANAGE WORLDS** shows each world's status and player count live.
- The row's right-click teleports you in without claiming a session, which is how an admin watches.
- `%exyliasandbox_total_players%` and `%exyliasandbox_queue_total%` put both numbers on a lobby board.
- The command whitelist in `config.yml` decides what a player may run while inside a sandbox world or
  while queued. Everything else is refused with `general.command-blocked` unless they hold
  `exyliasandbox.bypass.blocked-commands`.
