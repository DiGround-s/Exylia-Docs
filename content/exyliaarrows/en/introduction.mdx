title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaArrows is and what it ships with."
---

ExyliaArrows puts particle effects on projectiles: a trail behind an arrow in flight, a burst where it
lands, or both. Players pick theirs from a menu, and permissions decide which ones they may pick.

**65 effects across 6 categories** ship in `effects.yml`: elemental, magic, nature, cosmic, dark and
special.

## Five triggers

An effect names which moments it fires at, and one effect can name several:

| Trigger | Fires |
|---|---|
| `LAUNCH` | The instant the projectile leaves the player. |
| `TRAIL` | Repeatedly while it flies. |
| `HIT` | Wherever it lands, whatever it hit. |
| `HIT_ENTITY` | Only when it hits a living thing. |
| `HIT_BLOCK` | Only when it hits a block. |

`fire_trail` is written with `TRAIL` and `HIT`, which is the common pair: flames behind the arrow, a
burst where it lands.

<Callout type="note" title="HIT and the two specific ones stack">
`HIT` fires on any landing. Adding `HIT_ENTITY` on top does not double it — the effect plays if any of
its triggers matches. Use `HIT_ENTITY` alone when you want something that only happens on a person.
</Callout>

## Not only arrows

By default any launched projectile carries the effect. Turn that off and it falls back to a list —
`ARROW`, `SPECTRAL_ARROW`, `TIPPED_ARROW`, `EGG`, `SNOWBALL`, `WIND_CHARGE`, `ENDER_PEARL` — and an
individual effect can name its own list instead.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/arrows` shows the categories, then the effects inside one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pick one">
    The choice is stored in the database and survives a restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They shoot">
    Whatever the effect's triggers say happens, happens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Visibility

Every player chooses what they see, and the choice is theirs alone: `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or
`OTHERS_ONLY`. `/arrows toggleParticles` cycles it without opening anything.
