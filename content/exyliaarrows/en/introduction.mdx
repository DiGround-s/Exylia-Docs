---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaArrows is: 120 projectile effects with three moments each, drawn with display entities."
---

ExyliaArrows draws an effect on a **shot**. Not one effect: a shot has three moments and they are
nothing alike — the flash at the bow, the trail along the flight, and the impact it ends in. Every
effect in the catalogue declares all three and they are compiled apart, because their budgets have
nothing in common.

**120 effects in six categories** ship in `effects.yml`, built out of **display entities** — real
blocks and items that turn, fall and grow, animated by each viewer's own client — with particles
around them for light and smoke. The rule the catalogue holds itself to is that every moment of every
effect puts at least one solid object on screen.

## The three moments

| Trigger | When it plays | What it can afford |
|---|---|---|
| `launch` | The shot leaving the bow. | One flash. Optional, short, quiet. |
| `trail` | Along the flight, every `trail-interval` ticks. | One or two small displays plus a particle line. No sound. This is the one with a budget. |
| `hit` | Wherever it lands, whatever it hit. | The payoff: under a second, a dozen displays at most, one or two quiet sounds. |
| `hit-entity` | Only when it lands on something alive. | Falls back to `hit` when it is not declared. |
| `hit-block` | Only when it lands on a block. | The same. |

An arrow lives about forty ticks and four archers are four arrows in the air, so whatever `trail`
writes is drawn on every one of those ticks, for every shot.

## Two ways to own an effect

| Way | Whose it is | Which server it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **The menu** | The player's: they pick one in `/arrows` and it plays on whatever they shoot. | Practice, PvP |
| **A bow** | The bow's: an effect token is dropped onto a bow and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. | Survival, economy |

`behaviour.mode` decides which is read — `player`, `bow` or `both` — and is covered in
[Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliaarrows/modes-and-tokens).

## The categories

| Category | Icon | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| `ember` | `BLAZE_POWDER` | Coals, fire and forge work. |
| `frost` | `BLUE_ICE` | Ice, snow and things that stop moving. |
| `storm` | `LIGHTNING_ROD` | Wind, rain and lightning. |
| `void` | `ECHO_SHARD` | Sculk, souls and what is left after. |
| `prism` | `AMETHYST_SHARD` | Crystal, starlight and colour. |
| `goofy` | `SLIME_BALL` | Jokes. Rubber chickens, pies and a sad trumpet. |

Twenty effects each. The whole list is in [Effects](/docs/exyliaarrows/effects).

## Which projectiles count

By default **every** launched projectile carries the effect — arrows, tridents, snowballs, eggs, wind
charges, ender pearls. `projectiles.all-types` turns that into a list, and an effect may name
projectiles of its own.

## Visibility is the observer's

| Mode | What they see |
|---|---|
| `ALL` | Every effect, theirs and everybody else's. |
| `NONE` | None at all. |
| `SELF_ONLY` | Only their own shots. |
| `OTHERS_ONLY` | Only other people's. |

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Folia supported. |
| Required | `packetevents` |
| Recommended | `ExyliaLib` (a soft depend: the loader installs it) |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI`, WorldGuard for the `arrows-effects` flag |
| Database | Always. The choice, the visibility and the favourites live in one table. |
