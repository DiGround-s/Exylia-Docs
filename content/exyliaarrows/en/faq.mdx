## Nothing plays when a player shoots

In order:

1. **Is the projectile allowed?** `projectiles.all-types` is `true` by default; with it off, only the
   types in `projectiles.default-allowed-types` — or the ones the effect names — carry anything.
2. **Is there an effect at all?** In `mode: bow` the menu choice is never read, and in
   `mode: player` a bound bow is never read.
3. **Do they own it?** A menu choice they no longer have the node for is taken off instead of played.
4. **Is anyone set to see it?** A player on `NONE` sees nothing, including their own.
5. **Is the trail ceiling full?** Past `trail.max-active` a shot still plays its launch and impact and
   simply leaves no trail.

## The menu says it is off

`behaviour.mode` is `bow`. On that server the menu would change nothing, so it refuses to open
rather than pretending.

## An effect I bound to a bow vanished

The bow carries an id. If the key in `effects.yml` was renamed, that id names nothing any more and
the effect does not play. Renaming a key is not free: it is also a different permission node.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission for it went away — a rank expiring, usually. A choice that can no longer be
justified is taken off rather than played. The node comes back, they pick it again.

## Does losing a permission break a bow?

No. A bound bow plays its effect for whoever holds it: the node is asked for when the token is
bound, not on every kill. That is what makes a bound bow worth trading.

## Can players see each other's effects?

That is each observer's own choice: `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or `OTHERS_ONLY`, set with
`/arrows visibility` or from the menu.

## Are the bodies real entities?

No. `[NPC]` draws a display: nothing can hit it, loot it or collide with it, and it disappears when
its `life` runs out.

## Does the bow lore get written to the item?

Never. It is added to the copy that leaves the server. Any plugin reading that bow finds the lore
it always had, and renaming an effect updates every bow on the server at once.

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes — that is what `effects.yml` is for. Twenty-five shapes, thirteen other tokens and the display
parameters are in [Sequences](/docs/exyliaarrows/sequences). A line that cannot be read is
reported on the console and skipped; the rest of the effect still plays.

## Effects lag during big fights

Displays are animated by the client, so they are cheap to move — but every line is still a packet per
viewer. Look at how many lines an effect has and at `count:` on any particle lines. `ticks:` spreads a
shape over frames instead of sending it all at once.

## Can a region turn effects off?

Yes. ExyliaLib registers a WorldGuard flag, `kill-effects`: denying it in a region stops effects
playing where somebody died inside it. On a server without WorldGuard the flag simply allows.

## Can a region turn effects off?

Yes. ExyliaLib registers a WorldGuard flag, `arrows-effects`: denying it in a region stops launch,
trail and impact effects inside it. On a server without WorldGuard the flag simply allows.

## Why is my trail missing but the impact fine?

Either the region flag above, or `trail.max-active` — a full lobby firing crossbows hits the ceiling,
and losing a trail is far less visible than losing the tick rate. A trail also stops after
`trail.max-duration` seconds, which is what keeps an arrow lost in an unloaded chunk from drawing
forever.

Yes, it declares Folia support.
