## The plugin does not start

`ExyliaLib` and `packetevents` are both required. Without either, the server disables it by name.

## The permissions do nothing

Check the spelling: it is `exyliarrows`, with one `a`, not `exyliaarrows`. That single letter is the
most common reason a correctly-written node grants nothing.

## The menu will not open

That needs `exyliarrows.command.arrows`. The same node also covers `reset` and `toggleParticles`, so
without it a player cannot turn their particles off either.

## Nothing happens when a player shoots

1. **Do they own the effect?** Its `permission` field, or `exyliarrows.effect.*`.
2. **Does the effect apply to that projectile?** An effect with `projectile-types` only fires on those,
   and with `projectiles.all-types: false` the config's list applies.
3. **Do its triggers cover what happened?** An effect written with only `HIT_ENTITY` does nothing when
   the arrow hits a wall.
4. **Is anyone set to see it?** A player on `NONE` sees nothing, including their own.

## The trail keeps drawing after the arrow lands

It should stop the moment the projectile stops being valid. If it lingers, the arrow probably landed in
an unloaded chunk and never fired a hit event — which is what `trail.max-duration` catches, ten seconds
by default. Never set that to zero.

## Trails lag the server

`trail.default-interval` is the global knob: `2` is a step every tenth of a second, `1` doubles every
trail at once. Prefer raising an individual effect's `trail-interval`, and keep trails to one or two
`[PARTICLE]` lines — a shape in a trail is a shape drawn every tick of the flight.

## Can I have an effect only on ender pearls?

Yes — give that effect a `projectile-types` list with `ENDER_PEARL` in it.

## Can I sell a whole category?

Not with one node: there is no category permission here. Either grant each effect's node, or give a
group of effects the same string in their `permission` field and sell that.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. The trail task is tied to the projectile, so it runs on that projectile's region.
