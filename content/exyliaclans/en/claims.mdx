---
title: "Claims"
description: "One rectangle per clan: the selection tool, the price, the two protection modes, reclaiming and the map."
---

A clan owns at most one claim: a rectangle in one world, stored as `minX`, `minZ`, `maxX`, `maxZ` and the
height it was made at. There is no vertical bound — a claim owns its columns from bedrock to sky.

## The selection tool

`/clan claim` gives the player a **golden hoe** and starts a session. It needs the `CLAIM_LAND` rank
permission.

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left click a block | Corner 1 |
| Right click a block | Corner 2 |
| Shift + click in the air | Pay and confirm |
| `/clan claim` again | Cancel |

Glass towers topped with a diamond block mark each corner, drawn only for the player selecting. They are
packet-level: no block is ever placed in the world.

The tool cannot be dropped, cannot be stored (opening any inventory is blocked while a session is
running), does not drop on death, and is stripped from the inventory on join, so a stale one can never
follow a player around.

## Price

```
price = base-price + (X × Z) × price-per-block
```

With the defaults — 500 and 1.0 — a 20 × 20 rectangle costs 500 + 400 = **900**. The money comes out of
the **clan bank**, never out of the player's own balance.

## What gets a claim refused

| Refusal | Setting |
|---|---|
| The clan already has a claim | — |
| The two corners are in different worlds | — |
| Area below the minimum | `min-area`, default 25 |
| Area above the maximum | `max-area`, default 10 000 |
| Overlaps a WorldGuard region | needs WorldGuard installed |
| Overlaps another clan's claim | — |
| Within N blocks of another clan's claim | `min-separation`, default 5; `0` disables |
| The bank cannot pay | — |

## Protection mode

```yaml
claims:
  protection-mode: WORLDGUARD
```

| Mode | Who enforces it |
|---|---|
| `BUILTIN` | This plugin. It listens for block break, block place, right-click on doors and containers, bucket fill and empty, and item-frame breaking, and checks the claim on every one. |
| `WORLDGUARD` | A WorldGuard region named `clan_<clanId without dashes>`, created and resized with the claim. Every member is a region member; the leader is the owner. |

<Callout type="danger" title="`WORLDGUARD` without WorldGuard means no protection">
It is the shipped default. If WorldGuard is absent, no region is created and the built-in listener
stands down anyway — claims are drawn and paid for but protect nothing. Set `BUILTIN` before anybody
claims.
</Callout>

Under `BUILTIN`, who may do what inside a claim is decided by three rank permissions — `BUILD`,
`OPEN_DOORS` and `OPEN_CONTAINERS` — and by clan membership: a player from another clan is refused
outright, whatever their own rank grants. `exyliaclans.claim.bypass` skips all of it.

<Callout type="note" title="Explosions are protected in both modes">
The explosion handlers do not check `protection-mode`. Any block of a claim whose owner is **not**
raidable is removed from the blast list, under either mode. That is what makes going raidable mean
something.
</Callout>

## Raidable claims

When the owning clan's DTR drops below zero, its claim stops protecting anything:

- Under `BUILTIN`, every build, door and container check returns true for everybody.
- Under `WORLDGUARD`, the region's `BUILD` flag is set to `ALLOW`.
- Explosions are no longer filtered out.

When the DTR regenerates back above zero, the flag is cleared and protection returns. See
[DTR](/docs/exyliaclans/dtr).

## Reclaim

```
/clan reclaim
```

Redraws the rectangle **inside** the existing one. The tool is the same, with the current bounds shown as
orange glass towers topped with a gold block; a selection that leaves those bounds is refused.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Enabled by | `reclaim-enabled`, default `true` |
| Costs | `reclaim-fee`, default `0.0`, plus any increase in area price |
| Refused while | the clan is raidable |
| Refused if | the new area is below `min-area`, or the selection leaves the old bounds |

Because the new rectangle has to fit inside the old one, its area price is never higher — so in practice
the cost of a reclaim is exactly `reclaim-fee`. Reclaiming shrinks a claim; it never moves or grows one.

## Unclaim

```
/clan unclaim
```

Needs `UNCLAIM_LAND`, and is refused unless the DTR state is `NORMAL` — a clan cannot dodge a raid by
dropping its own land mid-fight.

<Callout type="warning" title="Unclaiming clears the clan home">
The home has to be inside the claim, so removing the claim removes the home with it, and both are
written to the log. Setting it again means claiming again first.
</Callout>

## The map

```
/clan map
```

Draws every claim within `map-radius` (200 blocks) as coloured pillars, visible only to that player:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green glass, emerald block | Your own claim |
| Blue glass, lapis block | An ally's |
| Red glass, redstone block | Anybody else's |

It refreshes every five seconds and switches itself off after two minutes, or when the player runs the
command again, changes world or logs out. With no claim inside the radius, nothing is drawn and the
player is told the radius that was searched.

## Entering and leaving

Crossing a claim border sends a message naming the clan that owns it, on the way in and on the way out.
A blocked action prints its own line, rate-limited to one every 1.5 seconds per player so a held-down
left click does not fill the chat.

## Settings

```yaml
claims:
  base-price: 500.0
  price-per-block: 1.0
  min-area: 25
  max-area: 10000
  pillar-duration-seconds: 10
  min-separation: 5
  map-radius: 200
  reclaim-enabled: true
  reclaim-fee: 0.0
  protection-mode: WORLDGUARD
```

`pillar-duration-seconds` is how long the corner towers stay up for everybody nearby after a claim is
made, which is separate from the selection preview.
