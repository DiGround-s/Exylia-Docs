## Grant the commands first

No player command works without its node. The shortest working setup is a wildcard on the `commands`
branch:

```yaml
default:
  - exyliaclans.commands.*

admin:
  - exyliaclans.admin
  - exyliaclans.admin.*
```

If you would rather grant them one by one, [Permissions](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions) lists all
thirty-four.

<Callout type="warning" title="`exyliaclans.claim.bypass` is not in that branch">
It is a separate node and it is not covered by `exyliaclans.commands.*`. Give it to staff only: it
ignores every claim protection check on the server.
</Callout>

## Make a clan

```
/clan create Vanguard
```

The name is checked against three settings: `clan-name-min-length` (3), `clan-name-max-length` (16) and
`clan-name-regex` (`^[a-zA-Z0-9_]+$` — letters, digits and underscores). Names are unique, compared
case-insensitively.

The clan is created with the three ranks in `default-roles` — **Leader** (weight 100), **Officer**
(weight 50) and **Member** (weight 1, the default one new members get). The founder is given the
heaviest rank and is recorded as the leader.

<Callout type="note" title="Being the leader is not the same as holding a rank">
The leader passes every clan-permission check outright, whatever their rank says, and outranks
everybody for the weight comparison. That is why a leader cannot simply `/clan leave` — they have to
`/clan transferleader` or `/clan disband` first.
</Callout>

## Fund the bank

```
/clan deposit 5000
```

The money comes out of the depositing player's own balance and needs the `DEPOSIT_BANK` rank permission.
The bank is what pays for land, so this comes before the claim.

## Claim the land

```
/clan claim
```

You are handed a **golden hoe**. While you hold it:

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left click a block | Set corner 1 |
| Right click a block | Set corner 2 |
| Shift + click in the air | Pay and confirm |
| `/clan claim` again | Cancel the session |

Glass towers with a diamond block on top mark the corners as you set them, and only you can see them.
The price is shown before you confirm: `base-price` (500) plus `price-per-block` (1.0) for every block
of `X × Z`. A 20 × 20 rectangle is 400 blocks, so 500 + 400 = **900**.

The rectangle is refused if it is smaller than `min-area` (25), larger than `max-area` (10 000),
overlaps another clan's claim, comes within `min-separation` (5) blocks of one, overlaps a WorldGuard
region, or costs more than the bank holds.

<Callout type="tip" title="One rectangle per clan">
A clan owns exactly one claim. To change its shape, use `/clan reclaim`, which lets you redraw it
**inside** the existing bounds. To move it, `/clan unclaim` and claim again — and note that unclaiming
also clears the clan home.
</Callout>

## Set the home

```
/clan sethome
```

While the claims module is on, this only works standing inside your own claim. The home then shows up as
a client-side waypoint for every member — and for allies, as long as the alliance lasts.

`/clan home` teleports there after a five-second warmup that is cancelled by moving or by taking damage.

## Fill the ranks

Open `/clan` and go to **Roles**. From there a clan with `MANAGE_ROLES` can create ranks, rename them,
set their weight, choose which one is the default, and tick permissions one at a time.

Weight is the whole hierarchy: a member can only kick, ban, promote or demote somebody whose weight is
strictly **below** their own, and can only set a rank's weight below their own.

## Recruit

Two menus, in opposite directions:

- `/lfp` — your clan posts what it is looking for, and reads the applications it receives.
- `/lfc` — a player with no clan posts what they play, and clans browse the list and invite them.

See [Recruitment](/docs/exyliaclans/recruitment).

## What to tune before opening

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| `claims.protection-mode` | Ships as `WORLDGUARD`. Wrong for a server without WorldGuard. |
| `claims.base-price` / `price-per-block` | Land price against your economy, not against the defaults. |
| `dtr.death-loss` / `regen-*` | How long a raid window stays open. |
| `settings.max-members` | 30 by default, before level bonuses. |
| `relational-placeholders.label-*` | The shipped labels are `MIEMBRO` and `NADA`. |
| `settings.chat.quick-*-prefixes` | `.` and `@` swallow the start of a normal chat line. |
