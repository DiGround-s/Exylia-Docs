## Wear a skin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself the item">
    `/armorskin give <you> blue_vex`. The command tab-completes every skin id, and takes an optional
    amount from 1 to 64.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it onto armour">
    Open your inventory, pick the skin item up with the cursor and click a piece of armour. The click
    is cancelled, the skin is applied and the item is consumed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the armour on">
    You see the skin in your paper doll and in F5; everyone tracking you sees it on your body. The
    piece is still whatever it was — check with `/data get entity <you> Inventory`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the skin off">
    `/armorskin remover <you>`, then click that same piece with the remover on the cursor. The skin
    comes back as a skin item and the remover is consumed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear the whole set">
    Skin all four pieces with the same skin and its set bonus starts: `inferno` plays a sound and
    surrounds you with flames until you take a piece off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Applying needs the skin's own permission">
By default a player must hold `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` to apply a skin and to be seen wearing it —
`exyliaarmorskin.skin.*` covers all of them, and both nodes default to `op`. Grant the node, or turn
`require-permission` and `hide-without-permission` off in `config.yml`. See
[Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Applying only works in your own inventory">
The click is only read inside the player inventory window — that includes the four armour slots.
Dropping a skin item onto armour lying in a chest does nothing.
</Callout>

## What can go wrong, and what it says

| Situation | Message key |
|---|---|
| The clicked item is not armour | `not-armor` |
| The skin does not list that piece | `unsupported-piece` |
| The armour already wears a skin | `already-skinned` |
| The remover was used on unskinned armour | `no-skin` |
| The skin id on the armour is no longer in `skins.yml` | `unknown-skin` |
| Removing with no free inventory slot | `inventory-full` — the skin stays on the armour and the remover is not consumed |
| The player lacks `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` | `no-permission` — nothing is written and nothing is consumed |

## Write your own skin

Add an entry to `skins.yml`. The key is the id stored on armour; everything else is what it looks
like:

```yaml title="skins.yml"
skins:
  ruby_ward:
    name: '{error}&lRUBY WARD'
    pieces: []            # empty means all four
    color: '#c0102a'
    trim-pattern: ward
    trim-material: redstone
    icon: WARD_ARMOR_TRIM_SMITHING_TEMPLATE
    animation: ''         # a preset from animations.yml, or rainbow / pulse / frames
```

Then `/armorskin reload`. The skin exists immediately, `/armorskin list` shows it, and anyone already
wearing a piece with `ruby_ward` on it sees the new colour without re-equipping.

<Callout type="tip" title="The id is what the armour stores">
Renaming an entry orphans every piece already wearing it: the armour keeps the old id, which no
longer resolves, so it shows its real look again. Change the `name`, not the key.
</Callout>
