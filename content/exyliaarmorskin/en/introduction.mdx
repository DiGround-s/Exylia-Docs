---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaArmorSkin is: a colour and a trim drawn over any armour piece with packets, while the real item keeps everything it had."
---

ExyliaArmorSkin puts a **skin** on a piece of armour. A skin is a colour, an optional trim and an
optional animation, and it is drawn on the way to the client: the netherite chestplate stays a
netherite chestplate on the server, and everyone sees blue leather with a vex trim.

The armour itself is never converted. Applying a skin writes **one value** into the item's data —
`exyliaarmorskin:skin`, the skin id — and nothing else changes. Enchantments, attributes, durability,
custom model data and any other plugin's data stay exactly where they were.

## What the client is shown

| On the real item | On the visual |
|---|---|
| Netherite chestplate | The leather piece of the same slot, in the skin's colour |
| Its trim, or none | The skin's trim, when the skin sets one |
| Its display name | The same name, or the vanilla name of the real material |
| Its lore | The same lore, then a blank line, then the `armor-lore` lines |
| Its enchantments | Hidden, but the glint is kept |
| Its durability | The real damage **and the real maximum**, so the bar reads true |
| Its attributes | Hidden, and never replaced by leather's |

## Who sees a skin

| Viewer | Sees |
|---|---|
| Everyone tracking the wearer | The skin, on the body |
| The wearer, in their own inventory and paper doll | The skin |
| The wearer **in creative** | The real armour — a creative client echoes its slots back to the server, so it must be shown the truth |
| Anyone looking at a skinned piece in a chest, hotbar or on the cursor | The real item with the skin's lore lines added |
| Everyone, when the **wearer** lacks that skin's permission | The real armour — the id stays on the item and the look returns with the node |

## What ships

Twenty skins in `skins.yml` — ten still colours and ten animated — and eleven animations in
`animations.yml`, built from three animation types: `rainbow`, `pulse` and `frames`. Each animated
skin also carries a **set bonus**: particles around the body and a sound once every piece it fits is
worn with it.

Each skin owns a permission derived from its id, `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>`, and by default a player
needs it both to apply the skin and to be seen wearing it.

## How a skin gets onto armour

There is no menu and no player command. A skin travels as an **item**: drag a skin item onto a piece
of armour in your own inventory and it is applied; drag a **remover** onto skinned armour and the
skin comes off and is handed back as the skin item again.

<Callout type="note" title="Everything is packets, so nothing is stored">
There is no database and no per-player file. The only state that survives a restart is the id written
on the armour, which is why a skin follows the item through chests, trades and other plugins.
</Callout>
