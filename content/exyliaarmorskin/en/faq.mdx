title: "FAQ"
description: "The questions that come up first: what happens to the real item, creative mode, other plugins, placeholders and performance."
---

## Does applying a skin change my armour?

No. One value is written into the item's persistent data — `exyliaarmorskin:skin`, the id — and
nothing else. The material, enchantments, attributes, damage, unbreakable flag, custom model data and
every other plugin's data stay as they were. Removing a skin clears that one value.

## Do enchantments and armour points still work?

Yes. The server never sees the leather: only the copy travelling to the client is leather. Protection
IV still reduces damage by Protection IV, and a netherite chestplate still gives netherite's toughness.

## Does the durability bar lie?

No. The visual carries the real damage **and** the real maximum as components, so the bar, the
tooltip and F3+H all read what the real piece has — 592 for netherite, not leather's 80. Unbreakable
armour shows no bar at all.

## Why do I see my real armour in creative?

A creative client sends its inventory slots back to the server, so anything drawn into them would be
written onto the real item. Rather than risk that, the wearer is shown the truth while in creative.
**Everyone else still sees the skin**, and switching back to survival restores the wearer's own view.

## Is the skin visible to other players?

Yes — to everyone tracking the wearer, immediately, without a relog. Viewers entering render distance
see it too, because the visual is applied to the equipment packets themselves.

## What can be skinned?

Helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots — anything Bukkit treats as an armour piece, of any
material. Mob heads and carved pumpkins are not armour pieces and are refused with `not-armor`.

An **elytra** is refused too, and it is never dressed by a wardrobe either: it sits in the chest slot
and answers to everything that usually means "chestplate", but drawing it as a chestplate would cost
the wearer their flight.

## Can two skins stack on one piece?

No. A piece that already wears a skin answers `already-skinned`; use a remover first.

## What happens to the skin when I die or drop the piece?

A skin applied with an **item** travels with the piece, because it *is* on the piece: a skinned
chestplate in a chest, in a trade or on the ground is still skinned when it comes back. A **wardrobe**
choice never touches the armour at all — it stays with the player and lands on whatever they equip
next.

## Does it work alongside other armour plugins?

Yes, as long as they read the item rather than the packet. The skin is one extra data value, so a
plugin that trims armour, names it, enchants it or stores its own data keeps working. The reverse is
also true: the skin's lore lines are never written to the item, so nothing else can read them by
accident.

## Do players need a permission to use a skin?

Yes, by default. Every skin has a node derived from its id, `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>`, plus one per
slot underneath it (`exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>.helmet`), and both
`require-permission` and `hide-without-permission` ship as `true`: without the node a player cannot
apply the skin, and armour already wearing it is shown as the real piece. Turn either switch off in
`config.yml` to go back to "the item is the permission". See
[Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).

## I revoked a node and the player still sees the skin

The check runs when the piece is reconciled — on equip, join, respawn, `/armorskinsadmin refresh` and
`/armorskinsadmin reload` — not on a timer. `/armorskinsadmin refresh <player>` applies it immediately. Nothing
is ever removed from the item, so granting the node back restores the look.

## What is a set bonus?

An effect that plays only when a player wears **every piece a skin fits** with that skin: a sound or
title on completion, something ambient around the body while it holds, and a moment when it breaks.
The ten animated skins ship one. See [Effects](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/effects#set-bonus).

## Are there placeholders?

No. This plugin registers no PlaceholderAPI expansion. PlaceholderAPI is a `softdepend` only so that
`%placeholders%` **you** write into the item names, lore and messages resolve.

## Is there a menu?

Yes: `/wardrobe`, one screen that dresses the player instead of the armour. It is on while
`behaviour.mode` is `wardrobe` or `both`; on an `item` server it refuses to open and says so. See
[The wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/wardrobe).

## What is the difference between a skin item and the wardrobe?

A skin item writes onto the **armour**, so the look is traded, dropped and lost with the piece. A
wardrobe choice is stored against the **player**, so it survives death and lands on whatever they
equip. In `both` the armour wins: a skinned piece keeps its own skin, and the wardrobe fills in the
bare ones.

## Can a skin item be eaten, thrown or placed?

No. This plugin's items are inert: whatever material they are drawn as, using them does nothing. They
can still be picked up, dropped, traded and clicked in a menu — they simply refuse to be spent as the
item they look like.

## Does it need a resource pack?

No. Everything shown is vanilla: dyed leather, vanilla trims and vanilla items.

## What does it cost to run?

Nothing per tick for a still skin. Rewriting happens on three packet types, and only while somebody
is wearing a skin. An animated skin adds one timer per worn animated piece, and a frame that did not
change colour sends nothing; an `ambient` block adds one timer per worn piece that declares one.

## Does it run on Folia?

Yes. `folia-supported: true`, and every timer is bound to the player's entity.

## A skin disappeared after I edited the file

The id changed. The armour keeps the id it was given, so renaming an entry orphans every piece
wearing it — the piece shows its real look and a remover answers `unknown-skin`. Restore the key and
everything comes back.
