## The plugin does not start

`ExyliaLib` and `packetevents` are both required. Without either, the server disables it by name.

## The menu opens but everything is locked

Patterns and materials are separate permissions, and a trim needs both. Granting every pattern with no
material grants nothing usable. See [Commands and permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/commands).

## The trim does not appear on the armour

It is applied when armour is equipped. Re-equip the piece, or check that the player still has
permission for both halves of what they chose — a selection they cannot use is not applied.

## A player lost their trim after their rank expired

That is `validate-permissions-on-armor-equip`, on by default: a selection the player can no longer use
is deleted the next time they equip armour. Turn it off and the choice stays stored and comes back when
the rank does.

## Does it consume smithing templates?

No. Nothing is consumed and no smithing table is involved — the trim is applied to the item directly
from the stored selection.

## Does it overwrite a trim somebody smithed themselves?

Only on a piece where they have chosen something in the menu. A piece with no stored selection is left
exactly as it is, real vanilla trim included.

## Can I translate the menus?

Yes. `trims.yml` holds the display names and the three files under `menus/` hold every piece of text
the screens draw. Edit them in place; nothing else needs to know.

## Can I add my own patterns?

No. The keys in `trims.yml` are vanilla trim ids that the game applies; the file names and draws them,
it does not define them. What you can change is `name` and `material`.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
