## The plugin will not start

`packetevents` is required. `ExyliaLib` is a soft depend: if it is missing, the loader installs it on
the first start.

## The menu opens but everything is locked

A preset needs its node; a free combination needs three — the tab, the pattern and the material. See
[Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions).

## `/armortrim` says the menu is off

You are on `mode: item`. In that mode there is no per-player choice and no screen: trims travel as
items. Switch to `menu` or `both` and reload.

## The trim does not show

| Check | Detail |
|---|---|
| The mode | `item` ignores the menu choice; `menu` ignores what is engraved on the armor. |
| The permission | With `hide-without-permission` on, a trim you may no longer use stops being drawn. |
| Creative | A creative client sees its real armor. Everyone else still sees your trim. |
| The value | A preset deleted from `trims.yml` is not drawn until the entry comes back. |

If it still does not show, `/armortrimsadmin refresh <player>` recomputes and re-sends.

## Can armor be dropped and the trim lost?

It depends on the mode. An **item** trim lives on the piece, so it travels with it and is lost with it.
A **menu** trim belongs to the player and is applied again to whatever they put on next.

## Is the armor touched?

In menu mode, no: the engraving is drawn on the way to the client and the real item never carries it.
In item mode **one value** is written — `exyliaarmortrims:trim` — and nothing else: enchantments,
attributes, durability, custom model data and other plugins' data are untouched.

## Does it overwrite a hand-forged trim?

It does not erase it: the vanilla armor keeps its own on the server. What gets drawn is this plugin's
trim, and vanilla's upgrade line is hidden on the visual.

## Can I add my own patterns?

You can offer any the game has by listing them in `trims.yml`, and rename and draw them however you
like. What you cannot do is invent a pattern the server's registry does not have.

## Can I translate it?

Yes. `trims.yml` holds pattern, material and preset names; `config.yml` holds the messages and the
menu wording; `menus/trims.yml` holds every line on the screen.

## Does it use up smithing templates?

No, and there is no smithing table involved. If you want the look to cost something, leave
`consume-trim-item` on and sell the trim items.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support.
