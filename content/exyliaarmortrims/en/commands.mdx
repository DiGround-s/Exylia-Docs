## Player

| Command | Aliases | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/armortrim` | `/armortrims`, `/trims`, `/trim` | `exyliaarmortrims.command.armortrim` | Opens the trim menu. |

With `mode: item` the menu is off and the command answers with the `menu-disabled` message.

## Admin

Everything under `/armortrimsadmin` — aliased `/armortrimadmin` — needs `exyliaarmortrims.admin`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/armortrimsadmin give <player> <trim> [amount]` | Gives trim items. Amount is 1 to 64. |
| `/armortrimsadmin remover <player> [amount]` | Gives removers. |
| `/armortrimsadmin set <player> <trim> [piece]` | Writes that trim into their menu choice. With no piece, every piece it fits. |
| `/armortrimsadmin clear <player> [piece]` | Clears one piece's choice, or all of them. |
| `/armortrimsadmin open <player>` | Opens the menu for them. |
| `/armortrimsadmin list` | Lists the registered presets with their ids. |
| `/armortrimsadmin refresh [player]` | Recomputes and re-sends the look. With no player, everybody. |
| `/armortrimsadmin reload` | Re-reads `config.yml`, `trims.yml` and the menu. |

`<trim>` takes either a preset id — `shogun` — or a combination — `sentry/netherite`. Tab completion
suggests the presets.

`[piece]` is `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings` or `boots`.

<Callout type="note" title="set does not ask about permissions">
`set` writes the choice **even when the player does not hold the node**, which is what an
administrator means by "set": running it twice leaves the same result, where the menu's click would
put the trim on and take it off again.

If `hide-without-permission` is also on, the player will not see what you gave them until they have
the permission.
</Callout>

## What reload covers

It re-reads `config.yml` and `trims.yml`, recompiles `menus/trims.yml`, rebuilds every trim's visual
and refreshes everybody online. A mode changed from `item` to `menu` or `both` brings up the database
and the menu right then.

It does not reload ExyliaLib — that is `/exylialib reload` — and does not reopen the database
connection.
