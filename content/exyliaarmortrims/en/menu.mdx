---
title: "The menu"
description: "One screen: armor along the top, three faces in the grid, all of it editable in menus/trims.yml."
---

`/armortrim` opens a single window. What is being engraved sits along the top; what can go on it sits
below. Nothing is reopened between steps, so the armor never moves and a half-built choice is never
lost.

## The two lists

| Section | Default slots | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `targets` | `2,3,4,5,6` | FULL SET and the four pieces. The marked row is what is being engraved. |
| `grid` | `10-16,19-25,28-34,37-43` | What can go on it: presets, patterns or materials, depending on the tab. |

The grid has **three faces and one section**, because a slot belongs to one list. The tab swaps what
it holds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="PRESETS">
    Every preset from `trims.yml`, each under its own name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CUSTOM → patterns">
    The tab always lands on the patterns, never on the materials of a pattern chosen minutes ago:
    switching tabs is how a player starts over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CUSTOM → materials">
    Once the pattern is picked, the grid moves to the materials, already previewed with that pattern.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Presets and materials are drawn **on the armor the player is really wearing**: a netherite chestplate
previews the trim on netherite. The **patterns** are drawn as their own smithing template instead —
at that point no material has been picked, and the same shape on a helmet the size of an inventory
icon is one picture eighteen times over. Swap `%entry_icon%` back to `%preview_material%`, with an
`armor_trim` block, to preview them on armor too.

## Fixed buttons

| Button | Slot | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| PRESETS | `0` | Opens the presets face. |
| CUSTOM | `8` | Opens the patterns. With `require-permission` on it needs `exyliaarmortrims.custom`. |
| BARE EVERYTHING | `45` | Takes the trim off all four pieces. |
| CLOSE | `49` | Closes. |
| Previous / next | `48` / `50` | Grid pagination. |

The two tabs are **one button each**, not two drawings of the same slot: each shows
`%presets_status%` or `%custom_status%`, which come from `menu.tab-open` and `menu.tab-closed` in
`config.yml`.

## What a click does

| Where | Click | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A piece along the top | Left | Engraves that piece from now on. |
| A piece along the top | Right | Takes its trim off. |
| A row in the grid | Left | Puts that trim on, or takes it off when it was already the one worn. |

Wearing something on FULL SET engraves **only what the player may wear**: a rank holding one piece of
a preset gets that piece rather than a flat refusal.

## Templates

The first template of a list is what a row in no particular state uses, so `item_template` goes first.

| List | Templates |
|---|---|
| `targets` | `item_template`, `focused_template`, `set_template`, `focused_set_template` |
| `grid` · presets | `preset_template`, `preset_selected_template`, `preset_unsupported_template`, `preset_locked_template` |
| `grid` · patterns | `pattern_template`, `pattern_selected_template`, `pattern_locked_template` |
| `grid` · materials | `material_template`, `material_selected_template`, `material_locked_template` |

`unsupported` is a preset that does not fit the focused piece; `locked` is one the player may not use.
A locked entry **looks locked** rather than simply not being there.

## Placeholders in the file

| Where | Available |
|---|---|
| `targets` | `%target%`, `%target_name%`, `%piece%`, `%piece_material%`, `%trim_name%`, `%trim_pattern%`, `%trim_material%`, `%engraved%`, `%pieces%` |
| `grid` | `%entry_id%`, `%entry_name%`, `%entry_icon%`, `%preview_material%`, `%trim_pattern%`, `%trim_material%`, `%entry_worn%`, `%entry_worn_name%`, plus `%entry_pieces%` on presets |
| Anywhere | `%tab%`, `%target%`, `%target_name%`, `%pattern%`, `%pattern_name%` |

## Actions

Six verbs, in the `armortrim` namespace:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `armortrim:open` | Opens the menu. |
| `armortrim:focus <piece\|set>` | Changes what is being engraved. |
| `armortrim:tab <presets\|custom>` | Swaps the face of the grid. |
| `armortrim:pattern <id>` | Takes the first half and moves to the materials. |
| `armortrim:wear <id\|pattern/material>` | Puts that trim on, or takes it off. |
| `armortrim:clear <piece\|set>` | Takes the trim off one piece or all of them. |

Each acts on the window the click came from, so a player with the menu open twice cannot move the
wrong one.

## Wording

The few strings a template cannot spell out slot by slot live in `config.yml`:

```yaml title="config.yml"
menu:
  full-set: "{primary}&lFULL SET"
  helmet: "{primary}&lHELMET"
  chestplate: "{primary}&lCHESTPLATE"
  leggings: "{primary}&lLEGGINGS"
  boots: "{primary}&lBOOTS"
  empty: "{muted}Nothing"
  custom-name: "{primary}&l%pattern% {letters_black}» {secondary}&l%material%"
  tab-open: "{success}✔ You are here"
  tab-closed: "{warning}➥ Click to open"
```

<Callout type="note" title="No menu, no screen">
With `mode: item` the menu does not exist: `/armortrim` answers with `menu-disabled` and nothing
opens.
</Callout>
