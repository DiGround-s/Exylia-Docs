## The three modes

`behaviour.mode` decides where the trim that gets drawn comes from:

| Mode | Where it comes from | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `item` | Only what a trim item wrote onto the armor. | The trim is traded, lost on death and sold as an item. No database and no menu are opened. |
| `menu` | Only what the player picked in `/armortrim`. | It follows the player, not the armor. Trim items do nothing. |
| `both` | The armor's own trim, and the menu's choice for armor that carries none. | The default. |

In `both` the **armor wins**: an engraved piece is drawn with its own trim, and the menu's choice only
fills the bare pieces.

```yaml title="config.yml"
behaviour:
  mode: both
  consume-remover: true
  consume-trim-item: true
  require-permission: true
  hide-without-permission: true
```

## The trim item

An item carrying a trim inside it. It is applied by **dropping it onto a piece of armor in your own
inventory**: pick it up on the cursor and click the piece.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Armor only | Anything else is ignored, and the click carries on as normal. |
| One at a time | A piece that already carries a trim is refused: take it off with a remover first. |
| Supported pieces | A preset with a limited `pieces` list will not go onto a piece it does not fit. |
| Permission | With `require-permission` on the trim's node is needed; without it, anyone holding the item may use it. |
| Consumption | `consume-trim-item` decides whether it is used up. Off, the item is reusable. |

What is written onto the armor is **one value**: `exyliaarmortrims:trim`, the preset id or the
`pattern/material` pair. The vanilla trim and everything else are left alone; the engraving is drawn
on the way to the client.

<Callout type="note" title="A trim item is not the item it is drawn as">
Trim items and removers are inert: drawn as an ender pearl one cannot be thrown, drawn as food it
cannot be eaten. Whatever it looks like, it can only be dropped onto armor.
</Callout>

## The remover

Dropped the same way, onto a trimmed piece. The trim comes off and is **handed back as a trim item**
to whoever removed it.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| A free slot | Without a free inventory slot nothing happens and it says so: the armor is not touched until the item fits. |
| Consumption | `consume-remover` decides whether the remover is used up. |
| Unknown trim | A trim the registry no longer knows is refused rather than turned into nothing. |

Both items are drawn from `config.yml` — `trim-item` and `remover-item` — and are recognised by their
data, never by name or material, so renaming them breaks nothing.

## Permissions and the look

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| `require-permission` | Whether wearing a trim needs its node. Commands and the API are never limited by this. |
| `hide-without-permission` | Whether armor whose trim the wearer may no longer use is shown as it really is. |

With both on — the default — an expired rank stops seeing the trim but **loses nothing**: the value is
still on the armor and in their row, and the look returns with the node.

## Effects

One effect when a trim goes on and one when it comes off, for the menu and for the items alike:

```yaml title="config.yml"
effects:
  on-apply:
    sound:
      name: BLOCK_SMITHING_TABLE_USE
      pitch: 1.2
  on-remove:
    sound:
      name: BLOCK_GRINDSTONE_USE
```

They are full ExyliaLib effects: title, action bar, boss bar, sound, particles and fireworks.
