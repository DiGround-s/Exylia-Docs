---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaArmorTrims is and what it ships with."
---

ExyliaArmorTrims lets a player put armour trims on their gear from a menu, with no smithing table and
no templates consumed. The choice is cosmetic, stored per player and per armour piece, and reapplied
automatically whenever they equip armour.

**18 patterns and 11 materials**, which is every vanilla combination — 198 looks per piece.

| | |
|---|---|
| Patterns | Sentry, Dune, Coast, Wild, Ward, Eye, Vex, Tide, Snout, Rib, Spire, Wayfinder, Shaper, Silence, Raiser, Host, Flow, Bolt |
| Materials | Quartz, Iron, Netherite, Redstone, Copper, Gold, Emerald, Diamond, Lapis, Amethyst, Resin |

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/armortrim` opens the main screen, which leads to a pattern menu and a material menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pick per piece">
    Helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots are chosen separately — a gold-trimmed helmet over
    diamond-trimmed boots is a valid combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They equip armour">
    The trim is applied to whatever they are wearing, every time armour changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="The selection outlives the armour">
The trim is stored against the player, not against the item. Dying and getting fresh armour, or
switching sets between matches, reapplies it — which is what makes it a cosmetic rank perk rather than
a one-time item edit.
</Callout>

## What it costs the player

Nothing in game. No smithing table, no template, no material consumed. What limits it is permissions:
which patterns and materials a player may choose, optionally down to the individual armour piece.

That is what makes it sellable — see [Commands and permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/commands).
